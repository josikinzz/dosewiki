## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Dosage & Duration

### Oral

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 0.3 mL
- Light: 0.3 - 0.9 mL
- Common: 0.9 - 1.5 mL
- Strong: 1.5 - 3 mL Warning: Doses over 2 mL can induce heavy sleep.
- Heavy: 3+ mL

**Duration:**
- Total: 1 - 2 hours
- Onset: 3 - 10 minutes
- Come up: 5 - 15 minutes
- Peak: 30 - 45 minutes
- Offset: 15 - 30 minutes
- After effects: 1 - 3 hours

1ml is equivalent to 1.66 grams of the sodium salt of GHB (Sodium oxybate).

Because of these pharmacokinetic differences, GBL tends to be more potent and faster-acting than GHB, but has a shorter duration...

Tolerance will develop to the sedative-hypnotic effects within several days of continuous use. After cessation, the tolerance returns to baseline in 7 - 14 days.

## Source: Safer Party

## Dosage
The dosage depends on the degree of dilution and is even more delicate with GBL than with GHB, as not every body converts GBL into GHB at the same rate and in the same amount. GBL is a strong acid that can corrode the mucous membranes and must be heavily diluted with a non-alcoholic drink before drinking!

GBL should be dosed lower than GHB! To produce the same effect, you need about half as much GBL as GHB.
