## Source: TripSit Factsheets

A rare tryptamine derivative first synthesised by David Nichols. This drug, also known as lucigenol, has rarely been documented outside the lab.

*Note: As of writing this note, this substance has not been reported on dosages past 2mg. Caution should be given when dosing. The drug is near eqipotent to DOI.*

### Negative Effects

- sweating/chills
- muscle tension
- confusion
- insomnia
