## Source: Wikipedia

### Safety Evidence Insufficient

It is undergoing research for potential effects on stress, anxiety, and sleep, but **current clinical evidence is insufficient to confirm its safety or efficacy**.

### Duration Limitation

W. somnifera is **generally well tolerated for up to about three months** with mostly mild side effects.

### Pregnancy and Hormone-Sensitive Conditions

**It should be avoided during pregnancy or in people with hormone-sensitive conditions.**

**Use of W. somnifera is not recommended during pregnancy or in people with hormone-sensitive prostate cancer.**

### Liver Injury

**It has been linked to rare cases of liver injury, particularly in people with preexisting liver conditions.**

**W. somnifera can cause hepatotoxicity**, and its use as a supplement has been **linked to herb-induced liver injury**, particularly in people with preexisting liver conditions.

### Adverse Effects

W. somnifera may cause adverse effects if taken alone or together with prescription drugs. The most common side effects include **diarrhea, headache, sedation, and nausea**.

### Testosterone and Cancer Drug Interactions

**Ashwagandha may increase testosterone levels, which could adversely interact with cancer medications.**
