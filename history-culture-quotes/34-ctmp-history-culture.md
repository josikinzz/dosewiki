## Source: Wikipedia

3,4-dichloromethylphenidate (abbreviated as 3,4-DCMP, and incorrectly as 3,4-CTMP for the d,l-threo diastereomer) has been sold online as a designer drug.

### Legality

- **Canada:** As of November 2016 3,4-DCMP (aka 3,4-CTMP) is now included in Canada's CDSA as a schedule III substance.
- **China:** As of October 2015 3,4-CTMP is a controlled substance in China.
- **United Kingdom:** 3,4-CTMP was banned in the UK as a Temporary Class Drug from April 2015 following its unapproved sale as a designer drug.
- **Sweden:** Sweden's public health agency suggested to classify 3,4-CTMP as hazardous substance on 10 November 2014.
