## Source: Wikipedia

The drug acts as a serotonin 5-HT2 receptor agonist, including of the serotonin 5-HT2A receptor. It is closely structurally related to mescaline and to other scalines like escaline and allylescaline.

Methallylescaline has about 6 times the potency of mescaline, which has a much higher listed dose range of 200 to 400 mg.

Methallylescaline acts as a potent agonist of the serotonin 5-HT2A receptor. It also interacts with certain other targets, such as the serotonin 5-HT2C receptor.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

Methallylescaline likely acts as a 5-HT2A partial agonist. The psychedelic effects are believed to come from methallylescaline's efficacy at the 5-HT2A receptors. However, the role of these interactions and how they result in the psychedelic experience continue to remain elusive.
