### Pharmacology

The mechanism of action that produces 2C-T-2's [hallucinogenic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Hallucinogenic) and [entheogenic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Entheogen) effects has not been established in scientific literature; however, its primary psychedelic effects are more than likely a result of its efficacy at the [5-HT2A receptor](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) as a [partial agonist](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Agonist#Agonists) . This mechanism of action is shared by many other psychedelic [phenethylamines](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Phenethylamine) and [tryptamines](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Tryptamine) .

Athough no established scientific experiments have been performed to establish MAO-A inhibition of 2C-T-2, phenethylamine derivatives substituted with an alkylthio group at the 4 position (4-MTA, and the 2,5-desmethoxy derivative of [2C-T-7](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/2C-T-7) ) are known to act as selective [monoamine oxidase A inhibitors](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MAOI) . [*[citation needed](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Citation_needed)*] Furthermore, many compounds of the amphetamine-analogs of the [2C-T-x](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/2C-T-x) have been found to have highly selective MAO-A inhibition . Therefore, this substance may likewise have [MAOI](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MAOI) effects.

## Source: Wikipedia

The drug acts as a serotonin 5-HT2 receptor agonist, including of the serotonin 5-HT2A receptor.

### Toxicity

A potential risk of neurotoxicity from 2C-T-2 use (and 2C chemical series in general) has been shown in serotonergic and dopaminergic neurons, however the assay used concentrations unlikely to translate to recreational use of the compound (>50 μM). This has also been shown to be magnified in serotonergic-containing cells with combined use of 2C series drugs with alcohol, MDMA, and methamphetamine.

### Interactions

2C-T-2 is metabolized by the monoamine oxidase (MAO) enzymes MAO-A and MAO-B. Monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs) such as phenelzine, tranylcypromine, moclobemide, and selegiline may potentiate the effects of 2C-T-2. This may result in overdose and serious toxicity.

### Pharmacodynamics

2C-T-2 acts as a serotonin 5-HT2 receptor agonist, including of the serotonin 5-HT2A and 5-HT2C receptors. The mechanism of action that produces 2C-T-2's hallucinogenic effects is shown to be most likely a result from action as a serotonin 5-HT2A, 5-HT2B, and 5-HT2C serotonin receptor agonist, a mechanism of action shared by the psychedelic tryptamines and phenethylamines to varying degrees. 2C-T-2 has also shown to be a partial agonist of adrenergic receptors.
