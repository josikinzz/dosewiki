## Source: DrugBank

### Absorption

The immediate release version of hydromorphone reaches its peak concentration after 30-60 minutes while the extended-release version reaches the peak concentration after 9 hours.

### Half-life

The half-life of hydromorphone immediate-release is of 2-3 hour while the extended release can range from 8-15 hours.

Tolerance to many of the effects of hydromorphone develops with prolonged use, including therapeutic effects. After cessation, it takes about 3 - 7 days for the tolerance to be reduced to half and 1 - 2 weeks to be back at baseline.

## Source: Wikipedia

Hydromorphone 2 mg IV is equivalent to approximately 10 mg morphine IV.

Effects generally begin within half an hour and last for up to five hours.

Peak plasma levels usually occur between 30 and 60 minutes after oral dosing.
The onset of action for hydromorphone administered intravenously is less than 5 minutes and within 30 minutes of oral administration (immediate release).
