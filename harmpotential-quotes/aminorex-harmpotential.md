## Source: Wikipedia

### Deaths and Pulmonary Hypertension

It was withdrawn from the market after it was found to cause **pulmonary hypertension (PPH)**.

It was introduced as a prescription appetite suppressant in Germany, Switzerland and Austria in 1965, but was **withdrawn in 1972 after it was found to cause pulmonary hypertension in approximately 0.2% of patients, and was linked to a number of deaths**.

### Cardiac Damage Mechanism

**Activation of serotonin 5-HT2B receptors by aminorex**, either directly via agonism or indirectly via serotonin release, **has been implicated in the development of pulmonary arterial hypertension and cardiac valvulopathy** with the drug.

### Cocaine Contamination Risk

It can be produced as a metabolite of the deworming medication levamisole, **which is very frequently used as a cutting agent of illicitly produced cocaine**.

*[Note: Cocaine users may unknowingly be exposed to aminorex through contaminated products]*

### Schedule I Controlled Substance

In the United States, **aminorex is a Schedule I controlled substance**.
