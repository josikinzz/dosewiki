## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Oral
- **Threshold:** 1 mg
- **Light:** 3 - 5 mg
- **Common:** 5 - 15 mg
- **Strong:** 15 - 25 mg
- **Heavy:** 25 mg +

### Insufflated
- **Threshold:** 2 mg
- **Light:** 3 - 6 mg
- **Common:** 6 - 12 mg
- **Strong:** 12 - 20 mg
- **Heavy:** 20 mg +

### Duration
- **Oral Total:** 3 - 6 hours
- **Oral Onset:** 20 - 40 minutes
- **Insufflated Total:** 3 - 5 hours
- **Insufflated Onset:** 2 - 5 minutes
- **After effects:** 4 - 48 hours

Users should also avoid frequent redosing as it is reported to be relatively easy to overdose due to its potency and stimulating nature. ... The first half of its duration has been reported to produce primarily stimulating effects with minimal dissociation whilst the second half is primarily dissociating with minimal stimulation.
