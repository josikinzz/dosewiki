## Source: Wikipedia

Aleph-2 acts as a serotonin 5-HT2 receptor agonist. The drug is also a weak monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI), with IC50 values of 3,200 nM for monoamine oxidase A (MAO-A) and >100,000 nM for monoamine oxidase B (MAO-B). Aleph-2 produces anxiolytic effects in rodents.
