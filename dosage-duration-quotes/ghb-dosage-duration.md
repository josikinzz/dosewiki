## Source: Safer Party

Dose carefully! If GHB does not (yet) work, it is best to wait 2 hours and not add more right away. Often, overdosing is caused by adding more. Use an insulin syringe or graduated pipettes so that you can measure your unit of consumption accurately.

## Source: Wikipedia

The effects of GHB can last from 1.5 to 4 hours, or longer with large doses.

GHB tends to cause rapid unconsciousness at doses above 3500 mg, with single doses over 7000 mg often causing life-threatening respiratory depression...

Studies have shown that the maximum concentration of GHB in urine appears within 1 hour and rapidly declines thereafter.
