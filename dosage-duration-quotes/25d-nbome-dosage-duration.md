## Dosage & Duration

### Sublingual

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 300 µg
- Light: 300 - 800 µg
- Common: 800 - 1000 µg
- Strong: 1000 - 1200 µg
- Heavy: 1200 µg + The NBOMe series can be fatal at heavy doses.[1][2][3]

**Duration:**
- Total: 4 - 6 hours
- Onset: 20 - 40 minutes
- Peak: 1 - 2 hours
- Offset: 1 - 2 hours
- After effects: > 6  hours

### Insufflated

**Dosage:**
- Heavy: The NBOMe series can be fatal when insufflated.[1][2][3] It is strongly discouraged.

"Members of the NBOMe series have been linked to numerous overdoses and fatalities."
"It is strongly discouraged to insufflate (snort) or take higher doses of these compounds."

"It is worth noting that compounds of the NBOMe family are not orally active and should be administered sublingually by placing and letting it absorb into one's mouth over a period of 15-25 minutes. 25D-NBOMe can also be vaporized and inhaled to cause significantly more rapid and powerful effects as well as a shorter duration. However, this route of administration is highly advised against due to the difficulties of measuring and handling substances that are both active in the microgram range as well as having a low therapeutic index."

"Assuming the substance has been taken sublingually, the very first physical effect which a person will notice immediately after sublingual absorption is a strong, unpleasant, metallic chemical taste. This is accompanied by a very obvious feeling of general numbness of the tongue and mouth which can stay for up to an hour after the blotter paper has been consumed."
