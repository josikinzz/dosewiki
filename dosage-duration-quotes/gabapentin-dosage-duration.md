## Source: DrugBank

### Absorption

As this process [LAT1 transport] is saturable, the oral bioavailability of gabapentin is inversely proportional to the administered dose - the oral bioavailability of a 900mg/day regimen is approximately 60%, whereas a 4800mg/day regimen results in only 27% bioavailability. The Tmax of gabapentin has been estimated to be 2-3 hours.

### Half-life

The elimination t1/2 of gabapentin in patients with normal renal function is 5-7 hours.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Dosage & Duration

### Oral

**Dosage:**
- Bioavailability: 27% - 60%
- Threshold: 200 mg
- Light: 200 - 900 mg
- Common: 900 - 1500 mg
- Strong: 1500 - 2400 mg
- Heavy: 2400 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 6 - 10 hours
- Onset: 30 - 120 minutes
- Peak: 120 - 180 minutes

The decreasing bioavailability of gabapentin can be lessened by taking lower doses more often instead of higher doses less frequently. As a general rule, using any more than 250-300 mg of gabapentin every 30-45 minutes will result in wasting a significant portion of the total dose...

Tolerance will develop to the anxiolytic effects with prolonged continuous usage. After cessation, the tolerance returns to baseline in 7-14 days.

## Source: Wikipedia

The oral bioavailability of gabapentin is approximately 80% at 100 mg administered three times daily ... but decreases to 60% at 300 mg ... and 27% at 1,600 mg...

Gabapentin at a low dose of 100 mg has a Tmax (time to peak levels) of approximately 1.7 hours, while the Tmax increases to 3 to 4 hours at higher doses.

The elimination half-life of gabapentin is 5 to 7 hours. ... its duration of action orally is 2 to 4 hours and via intravenous or intramuscular injection is 60 minutes.
