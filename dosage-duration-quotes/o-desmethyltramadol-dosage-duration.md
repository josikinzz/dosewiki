## Source: The Drug Classroom

**Oral Dose**
- Light: 15 – 30 mg
- Common: 30 – 50 mg
- Strong: 50 – 70+ mg

**Timeline (Oral)**
- Total: 5 – 7 hours
- Onset: 00:30 – 01:00

O-DSMT bypasses [CYP2D6 conversion] and should give a more consistent effect profile if pure opioid-like properties are what the user desires. ... It does not usually produce a substantial “rush” of euphoria ... but it can reliably offer a relaxed, chill, and pleasantly sedated state.
