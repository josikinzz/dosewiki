## Source: DrugBank

### Background
Zolpidem, also known as Ambien, is a hypnotic drug... This drug decreases the time to fall asleep (sleep latency), increases the duration of sleep, and decreases the number of awakenings during sleep... It is available in both immediate acting and extended release forms.

### Absorption
Average peak zolpidem concentrations (Cmax) were 59 and 121 ng/mL [for 5 and 10 mg], respectively, occurring at a mean time (Tmax) of 1.6 hours for both doses.

### Half-life
The average zolpidem elimination half-life was 2.6 and 2.5 hours, for the 5 and 10 mg tablets, respectively.

## Source: Wikipedia

Zolpidem is a nonbenzodiazepine, or Z-drug... it decreases the time to sleep onset by about fifteen minutes and at larger doses helps people stay asleep longer. ... It generally has a half-life of two to three hours. ... Zolpidem is associated with complex sleep behaviors (CSBs), defined as activities performed during sleep followed by amnesia.
