## Source: DrugBank

### Absorption
After an oral dose, memantine is well absorbed. Its peak drug concentrations are attained in about 3-7 hours. Memantine shows linear pharmacokinetics when given at normal therapeutic doses.

### Half-life
The terminal elimination half-life of memantine ranges from 60 to 80 hours in humans.
