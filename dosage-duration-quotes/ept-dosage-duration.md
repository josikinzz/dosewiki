## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Dosage & Duration

### Insufflated

**Dosage:**
- Light: 20 - 40 mg
- Common: 40 - 80 mg
- Strong: 80 - 110 mg
- Heavy: 110 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 2 - 4 hours
- Onset: 5 - 20 minutes
- After effects: 1 - 3 hours

Initial reports describe the effects of EPT as mild and indistinct compared to structurally similar base tryptamines such as DMT, DET and DPT... Of these, it is reported to be most similar to DPT, albeit less potent and with a less immersive headspace.

Tolerance to the effects of EPT appears to form almost immediately after ingestion. After that, it takes about 3 days for the tolerance to be reduced to half and 7 days to be back at baseline (in the absence of further consumption).
