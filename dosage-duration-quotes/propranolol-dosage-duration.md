## Source: Wikipedia

Propranolol appears in the blood after 30 minutes and has a maximum effect between 60 and 90 minutes when taken orally. ... The elimination half-life of propranolol ranges from 2.8 to 8 hours ... with a typical half-life of around 4 hours. The duration of action of a single oral dose is longer than the half-life and may be up to 12 hours if the single dose is high enough (e.g., 80 mg).
