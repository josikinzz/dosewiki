### Fatal Overdose Risk with Depressants

**"Fatal overdose may occur when thienodiazepines are combined with other depressants such as opiates and/or alcohol."**

### Overdose Medical Emergency

**"Thienodiazepine overdose is a medical emergency that may lead to a coma, permanent brain injury or death if not treated promptly and properly."**

### Extreme Addiction Potential

**"Thienzodiazepines are extremely addictive."**

### Withdrawal Danger

**"Thienodiazepine discontinuation is notoriously difficult; it is potentially life-threatening for individuals using regularly to discontinue use without medical supervision."**

### Respiratory Depression

**"Thienodiazepines exert their effects through potentiating GABA, which can result in respiratory depression especially when combined with other depressants."**
