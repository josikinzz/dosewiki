## Source: Wikipedia

The dose range of MDA given in Alexander Shulgin's book PiHKAL (Phenethylamines I Have Known and Loved) and other sources is 80 to 160 mg. A wider recreational dose range for MDA of 20 to 200 mg or more, with a typical dose estimate of 90 mg...

The duration of MDA is about 5 to 8 hours and is about 2 hours longer than that of MDMA (3–6 hours). Shulgin originally gave a duration of MDA of 8 to 12 hours in PiHKAL, but he later revised this down to only 3 to 6 hours.
