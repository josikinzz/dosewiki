## Abuse

"Due to its fleeting duration and dopaminergic effects, 3-MMC is highly addictive and commonly abused. Repeated dosing within a sitting is typical... Common self-reported doses range from 50 to 150 mg, up to single 500 mg doses."

"Users may dose repeatedly in order to extend the drugs duration, leading to 0.5—2 gram \"sessions\" that can span an evening. Single-dose effects last from 30 to 60 minutes, typically peaking around 10-minutes post-dose."
