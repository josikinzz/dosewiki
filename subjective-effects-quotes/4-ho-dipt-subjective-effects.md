## Source: Wikipedia

The effects of 4-HO-DiPT have been reported to include introspective changes, insights and realizations, mild object distortion, slight color effects, rainbow halos around objects, very little in the way of closed-eye visuals, little in terms of psychedelic visuals or sensory changes in general, mild stimulation, mild elation, light tension, orgasmic enhancement, and powerful religious-esque experiences. Other effects included sensations of muscle loosening, leg tremors, chill-like sensations, and vague body malaise.

Alexander Shulgin ... has stated that he "truly doubt[s] that there is another psychedelic drug, anywhere, that can match [4-HO-DiPT] for speed, for intensity, for brevity, and [sensitivity] to dose, at least one that is active orally."
