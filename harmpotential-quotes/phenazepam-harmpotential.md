## Source: Wikipedia

### Side Effects

**"Side effects include hiccups, dizziness, loss of coordination and drowsiness, along with anterograde amnesia which can be quite pronounced at high doses."**

**"As with other benzodiazepines, in case of abrupt discontinuation following prolonged use, severe withdrawal symptoms may occur including restlessness, anxiety, insomnia, seizures, convulsions and death, though because of its intermediate half-life as well as that of its active metabolites, these withdrawal symptoms may take two or more days to manifest."**

### Drug Abuse and Dependence Risk

**"Usually, a course of treatment with phenazepam should not normally exceed 2 weeks (in some cases therapy may be prolonged for up to 2 months) due to the risk of drug abuse and dependence."**

**"To prevent withdrawal syndrome, it is necessary to reduce the dose gradually."**

### Contraindications and Special Caution

**"Benzodiazepines require special precaution if used in the elderly, during pregnancy, in children, alcohol or drug-dependent individuals and individuals with comorbid psychiatric disorders."**

**"Phenazepam should not be taken with alcohol or any other CNS depressants."**
