## Source: Wikipedia

βk-2C-B, bk-2C-B, also known as 4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxy-β-ketophenethylamine or as β-keto-2C-B, is a psychedelic drug of the phenethylamine and 2C family. It is the β-keto derivative of 2C-B.
βk-2C-B was first described in the scientific literature by Richard Glennon and colleagues in 2004. It was first encountered as a novel designer drug in Europe in 2013.

### Legal status

The drug has since become a controlled substance in Canada (October 2016), Germany, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.
