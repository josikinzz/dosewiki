### Pharmacodynamics

Maximum plasma concentration is reached within 30 minutes [after rectal administration]. ... Median Tmax [intranasal] of 17.3 (7.8 to 28.2) minutes. ... Median Tmax [IM] of 0.5 (0.25 to 0.5) hours.
