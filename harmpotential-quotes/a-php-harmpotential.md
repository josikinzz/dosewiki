### Addictive Behavior and Psychosis

It has **gained notoriety for its tendency to induce compulsive redosing and addictive behaviors** as well as **the ability to produce delusional states and psychosis when abused**.

### Unknown Toxicity

**Very little data exists about the pharmacological properties, metabolism, and toxicity of α-PHP.** It has recently become commonly marketed alongside research chemical stimulants.

### Cardiac Effects

**Abnormal heartbeat** - Due to the potency of its rush, α-PHP can cause **various uncomfortable or painful sensations in the heart, especially when abused** or used for extended periods. **Those with familial or personal heart issues are discouraged from using this drug** in its most potent routes of administration.

**Increased blood pressure** - α-PHP, especially when it is vaporized or injected, can lead to **sudden spikes of blood pressure that may manifest in an extremely uncomfortable "exploding heart" sensation**.

### Seizure Risk

**Seizure - α-PHP may lower the seizure threshold in some individuals**, especially when it is misused.

### Anxiety and Paranoia

**α-PHP can lead to states of extreme anxiety and paranoia when dosed too highly or frequently**, which is why eyeballing this substance is strongly discouraged. The anxiety and paranoia this compound produces shares many features of the paranoia that can be induced by methamphetamine or cocaine.

**Feelings of impending doom** - This typically is experienced when the substance is abused, but can happen at higher than needed doses, or the comedowns of binges.

### Psychosis

**α-PHP, like other strongly dopaminergic stimulants, can result in a stimulant psychosis** that may present with a variety of symptoms (e.g., paranoia, hallucinations, or delusions). A review on treatment for amphetamine, dextroamphetamine, and methamphetamine abuse-induced psychosis states that **about 5–15% of users fail to recover completely**.

### Deaths

**α-PHP has been reported to be the cause, or a significant contributory cause, of death in suicides and overdoses caused by combinations of drugs.**

### Tolerance and Addiction Potential

As with other short-lived highly dopaminergic stimulants, the **chronic use of α-PHP can be considered highly addictive with a high potential for abuse** and is capable of causing **psychological dependence** among certain users. When addiction has developed, **cravings and withdrawal effects may occur** if a person suddenly stops their usage.

### Dangerous Interactions

**Warning:** *Many psychoactive substances that are reasonably safe to use on their own can suddenly become **dangerous and even life-threatening** when combined with certain other substances.*

- **25x-NBOMe & 25x-NBOH** - Risk of **seizures, and heart failure in extreme cases**.
- **Alcohol** - Can result in blackouts and **severe respiratory depression**.
- **DXM** - Increased risk of panic attacks and hypertensive crisis, or **serotonin syndrome**.
- **MDMA** - Risk of **excessive blood pressure and heart strain (cardiotoxicity)**.
- **MXE** - May dangerously increase blood pressure and increase the risk of **mania and psychosis**.
- **Dissociatives** - Risk of **delusions, mania and psychosis**.
- **Stimulants** - May **increase one's heart rate and blood pressure to dangerous levels**.

## Source: Wikipedia

### Potency

Regarding the potency of alpha-PHP in the brain, chemist Michael H. Baumann of the Designer Drug Research Unit of the National Institute on Drug Abuse stated: **"alpha-PHP might be even more potent than alpha-PVP"**; this statement is based on laboratory tests of chemical reactivity.

### Legal Status

- **Sweden** - Suggested to classify α-PHP as **narcotic** on June 1, 2015.
- **China** - Controlled substance as of October 2015.
- **Netherlands** - Banned under the 1971 Vienna Convention on Psychotropic Substances as of October 29, 2021.
- **United States** - Schedule I controlled substance as of 2022.
