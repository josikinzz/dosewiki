## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Dosage & Duration

### Oral

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 0.1 mg
- Light: 0.1 - 0.2 mg
- Common: 0.2 - 0.3 mg
- Strong: 0.3 - 0.5 mg
- Heavy: 0.5 +

**Duration:**
- Total: 4 - 8 hours
- Onset: 20 - 40 minutes
- Peak: 1 - 2 hours
- Offset: 2 - 4 hours
- After effects: 1 - 12 hours

Similar to flubromazolam, this is an ultra potent benzodiazepine. A dose of about 0.1 mg (100 µg) is equivalent to 10 mg diazepam or 0.25 mg alprazolam.
