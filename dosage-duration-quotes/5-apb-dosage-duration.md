### Oral

- Strong: 80 - 100 mg
- Heavy: 100 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 5 - 8 hours
- Onset: 20 - 60 minutes
- Come up: 45 - 90 minutes
- Peak: 2 - 4 hours
- Offset: 1.5 - 3 hours
- After effects: 6 - 48 hours

"The oxygen in the furan ring is placed at the 5 position, which generally constitutes more stimulating effects than when the oxygen is placed at the 6 position, which is usually described as being more psychedelic in effects. 5-APB is commonly found as the succinate and hydrochloride salt. ...
