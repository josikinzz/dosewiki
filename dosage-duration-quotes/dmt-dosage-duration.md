## Source: Disregard Everything I Say

*Onset* : 15 - 60 seconds
*Duration* : 5 - 20 minutes
*Normal After Effects* : 15 - 60 minutes

*Threshold* : 2 - 5 mg
*Light* : 10 - 20 mg
*Common* : 20 - 40 mg
*Strong* : 40 - 60 mg
*Heavy* : 60 mg +

DMT is broken down by the digestive enzyme monoamine oxidase through a process called deamination and is therefore inactive if taken orally unless combined with a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI)...

In terms of tolerance, smoked DMT tolerance builds very rapidly but also decreases rapidly. You should wait at least one hour between trips to allow tolerance to go down.

## Source: Isomer Design (TiHKAL)

(with 20 mg, intramuscularly) “I began to see patterns on the wall that were continuously moving. ... Then kaleidoscopic images appeared to me in my inner eye, fantastically beautiful and colored.”

(with 75 mg, intramuscularly) "The third or fourth minute after the injection vegetative symptoms appeared... hallucinations consisted of moving, brilliantly colored oriental motifs... After 3/4 to 1 hour the symptoms disappeared..."

At 15 milligrams (the threshold psychedelic dose) nearly all subjects had visual hallucinations, but the auditory changes were rare. At 30 milligrams, the effects were overwhelming both in speed and in intensity. The rush, the freight-train as several subjects call it, was well underway well before the 45 second infusion was complete.

## Source: Safer Party

The perception of time changes greatly during intoxication. A few minutes can be perceived as very long and intense.

At high doses, such psychological side effects can be felt for days to weeks.

Dose carefully at first. If there is no effect, increase the dose the next time. Do not add more on the same day.

## Source: The Drug Classroom

## Dose

#### Inhalation (most common route of administration)
- Light: 10 – 20 mg
- Common: 20 – 40 mg
- Strong: 40 – 60 mg

#### Oral
- Common: 30 – 60 mg (DMT is typically inactive orally. This changes when it is combined with an MAOI.)

## Timeline

#### Inhalation
- Total: 10 – 15 minutes
- Onset: Under 30 seconds (The effects can begin in as little as 15 seconds. Peak effects arrive between 1 and 2 minutes.)

#### Oral
- Total: 3 -4 hours
- Onset: 00:30 – 00:45

Tolerance largely doesn’t occur with DMT. It can be used multiple times in a given day. One study found four closely-spaced DMT doses on the same day didn’t lead to subjective tolerance.

## Source: Wikipedia

When inhaled or injected, the effects last about five to fifteen minutes. Effects can last three hours or more when orally ingested along with a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI)...

Shulgin lists DMT's dose as greater than 350 mg orally, 60 to 100 mg by intramuscularly, subcutaneously, or via smoking, and 4 to 30 mg by intravenous injection. ... threshold effects occur at a dose of 30 mg and full effects occur at a dose of 50 to 100 mg by this route [intramuscular].

In terms of intravenous injection, a dose of 4 mg was indistinguishable from placebo, 8 mg produced physical effects but no psychoactive effects, 15 mg produced threshold psychedelic effects, and 30 mg produced strong psychedelic effects.

The onset of DMT has been given as 10 to 15 seconds smoked, within 2 to 5 minutes intravenously, and within 2 to 5 minutes intramuscularly. In addition, its duration is given as 5 to 20 minutes (average 12 minutes) via inhalation, less than 30 minutes intravenously, and 30 to 60 minutes intramuscularly.

The onset of oral DMT with an MAOI is within 1 hour and its duration is 4 to 6 hours.

DMT by continuous intravenous infusion (i.e., an intravenous "drip", also sometimes known as "DMTx") ... The dose range for this route is 0.6 to 1.8 mg per minute, with 0.6 mg/minute being a low dose, 1.2 mg/minute being an intermediate or "good effect" dose, and 1.8 to 2.4 mg/minute being a high or "ego dissolution" dose.
