## Source: Wikipedia

In his 1991 book PiHKAL ... Alexander Shulgin gave a dose range for TMA-6 of 25 to 50 mg and a duration of 12 to 16 hours. Threshold effects occur at 20 mg, a full hallucinogenic state occurs at 30 to 40 mg... The drug is said to have about 8 to 10 times the potency of mescaline.
