## Source: Wikipedia

### Controlled Substance Status

**It is a controlled substance; in the United States it is a Schedule I Narcotic controlled substance** with a DEA ACSCN of 9612 and an established manufacturing quota of 25 grams for 2022.

*[Note: As a potent opioid in the nitazene family, the following harm risks apply:
- High addiction and dependence potential
- Life-threatening respiratory depression
- Overdose risk, especially in opioid-naive individuals
- Dangerous combinations with other CNS depressants (benzodiazepines, alcohol)
- Severe withdrawal syndrome
- The nitazene class has been implicated in numerous overdose deaths globally]*
