## Source: Wikipedia

### Potent Synthetic Opioid

**Butyrfentanyl or butyrylfentanyl is a potent short-acting synthetic opioid analgesic drug. It is an analog of fentanyl with around one quarter of its potency.**

### No Legitimate Clinical Use

**Butyrfentanyl has no current legitimate clinical applications; however, it is being sold as a designer drug.**

### Life-Threatening Respiratory Depression

**Side effects of fentanyl analogs are similar to those of fentanyl itself, which include itching, nausea, and potentially serious respiratory depression which can be life-threatening.**

### Hundreds of Deaths

**Fentanyl analogs have killed hundreds of people throughout Europe and the former Soviet republics** since the most recent resurgence in use began in Estonia in the early 2000s, **and novel derivatives continue to appear**.

### Schedule I Controlled Substance

**As of May 2016, butyrfentanyl is a Schedule I controlled substance in the United States.**
