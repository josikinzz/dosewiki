## Source: DrugBank

Amphetamine, a compound discovered over 100 years ago, is one of the more restricted controlled drugs. It was previously used for a large variety of conditions and this changed until this point where its use is highly restricted. Amphetamine, with the chemical formula alpha-methylphenethylamine, was discovered in 1910 and first synthesized by 1927. After being proven to reduce drug-induced anesthesia and produce arousal and insomnia, amphetamine racemic mix was registered by Smith, Kline and French in 1935.

During World War II, amphetamine was used to promote wakefulness in the soldiers. This use derived into a large overproduction of amphetamine and all the surplus after the war finalized ended up in the black market, producing the initiation of the illicit abuse.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

Amphetamine (also known as alpha-methylphenethylamine, amfetamine, and speed) is a classical stimulant substance of the phenethylamine class.

Amphetamine was first synthesized in 1887 in Germany by Romanian chemist Lazăr Edeleanu who named it phenylisopropylamine. Its stimulant effects remained unknown until 1927, when it was independently resynthesized by Gordon Alles and reported to have sympathomimetic properties.

By 1934, Smith, Kline & French began selling the volatile salt form of amphetamine as the Benzedrine inhaler for use as a decongestant. During World War II, amphetamine and methamphetamine were widely used by both the Allied and Axis forces for their stimulant and performance-enhancing effects. As the addictive properties of the drug became known, governments began to place strict controls on the sale of the drug.

### Legal status

- **Germany:** Amphetamine is a Schedule III (Anlage III) controlled substance.
- **Switzerland:** Amphetamine is a controlled substance specifically named under Verzeichnis A.
- **United Kingdom:** Amphetamine is a Class B drug.
- **United States:** Amphetamine is a Schedule II controlled substance.

### History

Amphetamine was first synthesized in 1887 by Lazăr Edeleanu, although its central effects were not published until Gordon Alles resynthesized it in 1927. Alles was looking for a replacement for ephedrine. Benzedrine was the first brand of amphetamine, and was sold as an inhaler starting in 1932.

#### Legal status

- **United States:** Amphetamine is a Schedule II controlled substance.
- **United Kingdom:** Amphetamine is a Class B drug.
- **Australia:** Amphetamine is a Schedule 8 controlled substance.
- **International:** Under the Convention on Psychotropic Substances, amphetamine is in Schedule II.
