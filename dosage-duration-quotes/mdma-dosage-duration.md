MDMA is a widely abused drug classified as a hallucinogen and causes marked, long-lasting changes in brain serotonergic systems. ... It enters neurons via carriage by the monoamine transporters. Once inside, MDMA inhibits the vesicular monoamine transporter, which results in increased concentrations of serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine into the cytoplasm, and induces their release by reversing their respective transporters through a process known as phosphorylation.
