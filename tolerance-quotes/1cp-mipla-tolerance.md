## Source: psychonautwiki

### Dependence and abuse potential

Although no formal studies have been conducted, it is not unreasonable to assume that as with LSD itself, 1cP-MiPLA is not habit-forming and that the desire to use it can actually decrease with use.

Tolerance to the effects of 1cP-MiPLA is built almost immediately after ingestion. After that, it takes about 5-7 days for the tolerance to be reduced to half and 14 days to be back at baseline (in the absence of further consumption). 1cP-MiPLA presents cross-tolerance with all [psychedelics](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) , meaning that after the use of 1cP-MiPLA all psychedelics will have a reduced effect.
