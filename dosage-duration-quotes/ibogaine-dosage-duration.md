## Source: Wikipedia

Ibogaine ... typical dose range of 1,000 to 1,500 mg orally, with these doses producing hallucinogenic effects, and a duration of 18 to 36 hours. However, lower doses like 200 to 400 mg orally are also active. In addition, very low doses of ibogaine like 8 to 30 mg orally have been used and reported to produce stimulant effects.

The onset of the drug is 1 to 3 hours and peak effects have been described as being reached after 2 hours.

Each of these phases is described as having distinct qualitative effects.
The first phase is the acute or visionary phase, which onsets after 1 to 3 hours and has a duration of 4 to 8 hours; the second phase is the evaluative or introspective phase, which starts after 4 to 8 hours and has a duration of 8 to 20 hours; and the third phase is residual stimulation, which onsets after 12 to 24 hours and has a duration of 24 to 72 hours or longer.
