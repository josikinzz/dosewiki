## Source: TripSit Factsheets

### Classification

- **Categories:** benzodiazepine, depressant, **habit-forming**, tentative

### Additional Information

- **Avoid:** All other CNS depressants.

*[Note: Cloniprazepam is a prodrug for clonazepam. As such, the following harm risks from clonazepam apply:
- Physical and psychological addiction potential
- Life-threatening withdrawal with seizures if discontinued abruptly
- Dangerous respiratory depression when combined with other CNS depressants
- Tolerance development
- Memory impairment and amnesia]*
