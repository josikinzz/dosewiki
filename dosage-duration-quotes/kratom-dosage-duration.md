## Source: Wikipedia

The onset of effects typically begins within five to ten minutes and lasts for two to five hours.

At relatively low doses (1–5 g of raw leaves), at which there are mostly stimulant effects... At moderate to high doses (5–15 g of raw leaves), at which opioid effects generally appear...

Kratom is a botanical with a known addiction liability and, in vulnerable individuals, dependence may develop rather quickly with tolerance noted at three months and four- to ten-fold dose escalations required within the first few weeks.
