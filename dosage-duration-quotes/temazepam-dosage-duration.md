## Source: DrugBank

### Background
Temazepam ... is capable of eliciting a variety of actions including sedation, hypnosis, skeletal muscle relaxation, anticonvulsant activity, and anxiolytic action. ... Temazepam possesses a high potential for misuse and is genuinely capable of developing drug tolerance, physical dependence, and addiction in users.

### Absorption
The oral administration of 15 to 45 mg temazepam resulted in rapid absorption with significant blood levels achieved in 30 minutes and peak levels at 2-3 hours. ... Measurable plasma concentrations were obtained 10-20 minutes after dosing with peak plasma levels ... presenting approximately 1.2-1.6 hours (with a mean of 1.5 hours) after the dosing.

### Half-life
The terminal half-life determined for temazepam is recorded as being between 3.5-18 hours, with a mean of 9 hours.
