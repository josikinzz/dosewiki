## Source: Wikipedia

### Overdose

**A severe benzodiazepine overdose with bentazepam may result in coma and respiratory failure.**

### Adverse Effects

Adverse effects include **dry mouth, somnolence, asthenia, dyspepsia, constipation, nausea** and **drug-induced lymphocytic colitis has been associated with bentazepam**.

### Severe Liver Damage

**Severe liver damage and hepatitis has also been associated with bentazepam.** Whilst liver failure from bentazepam is considered to be rare, **liver function monitoring has been recommended for all patients taking bentazepam**.
