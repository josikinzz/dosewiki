## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Tolerance and addiction potential

Kava is known to produce a "reverse tolerance" in many users, in a similar fashion to substances like [kanna](/w/index.php?title=Kanna&action=edit&redlink=1) . This means that continued use is required in order to experience full effects. Some users do not experience this.

Kava is traditionally regarded as non-addictive especially in comparison to other chemically related drugs such as [benzodiazepines](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Benzodiazepines) . In some Pacific Island communities, there is some record of people who have a psychological addiction to kava and drink it like an alcoholic drinks [alcohol](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Alcohol) .
