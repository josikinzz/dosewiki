## Source: PsychonautWiki

Alprazolam (also known by the brand name Xanax) is a highly potent, short-acting [anxiolytic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Anxiolytic) substance of the [benzodiazepine](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Benzodiazepine) class. It is the most commonly prescribed and misused benzodiazepine in the United States.

Alprazolam was first synthesized by J.B. Hester at Upjohn (now part of Pfizer) in 1971. It was later approved by the FDA in 1981 for the treatment of panic disorder and generalized anxiety disorder (GAD).

### Legal status

- **Germany:** Alprazolam is a Schedule III controlled substance (Anlage III). It can only be prescribed by a physician.
- **Russia:** Alprazolam is a Schedule III controlled substance.
- **Switzerland:** Alprazolam is a controlled substance specifically named under Verzeichnis B.
- **United Kingdom:** Alprazolam is a Class C drug.
- **United States:** Alprazolam is a Schedule IV controlled substance under the Controlled Substances Act of 1970.

## Source: Wikipedia

Alprazolam, sold under the brand name Xanax, among others, is a short-acting tranquilizer of the triazolobenzodiazepine (TBZ) class, which are benzodiazepines (BZDs) fused with a triazole ring.
It was first synthesized by J.B. Hester at the Upjohn Company in 1971. It was patented in the United States in 1976 and was approved for medical use by the FDA in 1981.

### Society and culture

#### Recreational use

There is a risk of misuse and dependence among both patients and non-medical users of alprazolam; alprazolam's high affinity binding, high potency, and rapid onset increase its abuse potential.

#### Legal status

- **United States:** Alprazolam is a Schedule IV controlled substance under the Controlled Substances Act.
- **United Kingdom:** Alprazolam is a Class C drug (Schedule 4).
- **International:** Under the Convention on Psychotropic Substances, alprazolam is in Schedule IV.
