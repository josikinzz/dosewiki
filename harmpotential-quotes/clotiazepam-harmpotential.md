**Groups: Approved, Illicit**

Clotiazepam is a thienodiazepine, not approved for sale in the U.S. or Canada, but has been approved in the U.K. **It is a schedule IV drug in Canada.**

## Source: Wikipedia

### Side effects

**Side effects experienced with this product will resemble those of other benzodiazepines. Drowsiness and asthenia are common side effects. There has been a report of reversible hepatitis caused by clotiazepam.**

### Abuse

**Clotiazepam is a recognised drug of abuse.**

*[Note: As a thienodiazepine with benzodiazepine-like activity, the following harm risks from benzodiazepines apply:
- Physical and psychological dependence
- Tolerance development
- Withdrawal syndrome upon discontinuation (potentially including seizures)
- Respiratory depression when combined with other CNS depressants
- Memory impairment and amnesia
- Falls and cognitive impairment, especially in the elderly]*
