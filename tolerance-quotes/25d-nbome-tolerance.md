## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Tolerance and addiction potential

In terms of its addictive potential, 25D-NBOMe has not been studied formally but many users experience a self-regulating quality. Also, due to its immediate tolerance build up, which lasts up to 2 - 3 weeks after the experience, it is difficult to use this substance compulsively. 25D-NBOMe presents cross-tolerance with all [psychedelics](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelics) , meaning that after the consumption of 25D-NBOMe all psychedelics will have a reduced effect.
