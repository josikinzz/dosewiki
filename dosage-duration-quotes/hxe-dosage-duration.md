## Dosage & Duration

### Oral

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 30 mg
- Light: 30 - 60 mg
- Common: 60 - 100 mg
- Strong: 100 - 130 mg
- Heavy: 130 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 3 - 8 hours
- Onset: 20 - 60 minutes
- Come up: 30 - 60 minutes
- Peak: 2 - 4 hours
- Offset: 3 - 6 hours
- After effects: 1 - 12 hours

**Dosage:**

- After effects: 2 - 7 hours

Tolerance to many of the effects of HXE develops with prolonged and repeated use. ... After cessation, it takes about 3 - 7 days for the tolerance to be reduced to half and 1 - 2 weeks to be back at baseline.
