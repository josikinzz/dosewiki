## Source: Wikipedia

Methoxyacetylfentanyl, commonly known as MAF is an opioid analgesic that is an analog of fentanyl. Side effects of fentanyl analogs are similar to those of fentanyl itself, which include itching, nausea and potentially serious respiratory depression, which can be life-threatening.
