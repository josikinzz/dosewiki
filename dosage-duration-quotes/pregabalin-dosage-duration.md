## Source: DrugBank

### Absorption
After oral dosing administered in the fasted state, pregabalin absorption is rapid, and extensive. Pregabalin oral bioavailability is reported to be ≥90% regardless of the dose. Cmax is attained within 1.5 hours after single or multiple doses... Both Cmax and AUC appear to be dose proportional.

### Half-life
The elimination half life of pregabalin is 6.3 hours.
