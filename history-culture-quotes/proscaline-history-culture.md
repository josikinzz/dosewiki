## Source: PsychonautWiki

The synthesis of proscaline was first published by David E. Nichols in 1977. Around the same time, Alexander Shulgin sampled and evaluated the chemical at doses up to 60 mg, estimating its potency as five times that of mescaline. Shulgin later documented his findings in the 1991 book PiHKAL ("Phenethylamines I Have Known and Loved"). Proscaline appeared for sale on the online research chemical market in the 2010s.

## Source: Wikipedia

Proscaline was first synthesized and studied by Otakar Leminger in 1972. The drug was later synthesized by Alexander Shulgin and further described in his 1991 book PiHKAL (Phenethylamines I Have Known and Loved). It was encountered as a novel designer drug in Europe in 2013.
