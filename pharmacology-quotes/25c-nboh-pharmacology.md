## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Pharmacology

25C-NBOH has efficacy at the [5-HT2A receptor](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) where it acts as a potent [agonist](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Agonist#Agonists) .

This compound is pharmacologically unique when compared to other psychedelics due to the unusually high selectivity it displays for various [serotonin](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin) receptors. It is notable as one of the most selective agonist ligands for the 5-HT 2A receptor with a pEC 50 value of 9.43

It acts as a potent [serotonin](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin) [receptor](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Receptor) [agonist](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Agonist) with similar affinity to the better-known compound [25C-NBOMe](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/25C-NBOMe) at 5-HT 2A and 5-HT 2C receptors with pKis values of 9.4 and 7.8, respectively.

However, the role of these interactions and how they result in the [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) experience continues to remain elusive.

## Source: Wikipedia

25-C-NBOH (2C-C-NBOH, NBOH-2CC) is a derivative of the phenethylamine derived hallucinogen 2C-C which has been sold as a designer drug. It has similar serotonin receptor affinity to the better-known compound 25C-NBOMe.
