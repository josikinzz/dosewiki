## Source: Erowid

**Onset:** Effects can first be felt after around 60 to 90 minutes. Typically takes 2-4 hours for full effect to manifest when consumed orally.
**Duration:** 4-10 hours; longer with redosing.

Feeling of wellbeing, relaxation, slight disinhibition comparable to the effects of low-dose alcohol. ... Tolerance develops rapidly. Rebound anxiety can develop after 2-3 days of use.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Oral
- **Threshold:** 0.25 g
- **Light:** 0.5 - 1 g
- **Common:** 1 - 2 g
- **Strong:** 2 - 3.5 g
- **Heavy:** 3.5 g +

### Duration
- **Total:** 10 - 16 hours
- **Onset:** 1.5 - 3 hours
- **Come up:** 1.5 - 3 hours
- **Peak:** 3 - 4 hours
- **Offset:** 4 - 6 hours
- **After effects:** 6 - 24 hours

Due to the extremely long come up period relative to other substances, some users may experience an urge to redose out of the belief that it is weak or not working. This should be avoided to prevent overdose. ... Tolerance to the sedative-hypnotic effects develops within a couple of days of continuous use. After cessation, the tolerance returns to baseline in 7 - 14 days.
