## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Dosage & Duration

### Smoked
- Threshold: 2 mg
- Light: 5 - 10 mg
- Common: 10 - 20 mg
- Strong: 20 - 25 mg
- Heavy: 25 mg +

### Oral
- Threshold: 2 mg
- Light: 4 - 8 mg
- Common: 8 - 15 mg
- Strong: 15 - 25 mg
- Heavy: 25 mg + Heavy doses may result in psychosis and mania.[4]

### Insufflated
- Threshold: 1 mg
- Light: 2 - 5 mg
- Common: 5 - 10 mg
- Strong: 10 - 15 mg
- Heavy: 15 mg + Heavy doses may result in psychosis and mania.[4]

**Duration:**
- Total: 4 - 8 hours (Oral) / 3 - 5 hours (Insufflated)
- Onset: 30 - 90 minutes (Oral) / 5 - 30 minutes (Insufflated)
- After effects: 4 - 48 hours

"3-MeO-PCP may be more likely to cause mania, delusions, and psychosis than other dissociatives."
