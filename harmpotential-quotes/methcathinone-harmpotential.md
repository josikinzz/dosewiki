## Source: Wikipedia

### Addiction and Dependence Potential

**"is considered to be addictive, with both physical and psychological withdrawal occurring if its use is discontinued after prolonged or high-dosage administration"**

**"Methcathinone can be highly psychologically addictive, and can produce a methamphetamine-like withdrawal."**

**"In preclinical studies, methcathinone hydrochloride produces an abuse potential similar to that of the amphetamines."**

### Cardiovascular and Hypertension Effects

**"Increased blood pressure, risk of stroke or heart attack"**

**"it commonly causes hypertension (elevated blood pressure) and tachycardia (elevated heart rate)"**

**"Increased heart rate"**

**"Increased blood pressure"**

### Neurotoxicity from Illicit Manufacturing

**"Injecting this substance has been associated with symptoms similar to those seen in patients with Parkinson's disease (manganism) due to the compound manganese dioxide which is a byproduct of synthesis with permanganate."**

### Psychosis and Mental Health Effects

**"Chronic high dosage use may result in acute mental confusion ranging from mild paranoia to psychosis. These symptoms typically disappear quickly if use is stopped."**

### Binge Behavior and Long-term Consequences

**"Methcathinone binges resemble amphetamine binges in that the user may not sleep or eat, and takes in little in the way of liquids. The methcathinone binge is followed by long periods of sleep, excess eating, long-lasting nosebleeds (insufflation of methcathinone is corrosive to the nasal mucosa in the same manner as methamphetamine) and, in some cases, depression."**

### Nasal Damage from Insufflation

**"long-lasting nosebleeds (insufflation of methcathinone is corrosive to the nasal mucosa in the same manner as methamphetamine)"**

### Dehydration Risk

**"Users can easily forget to consume fluids leading to increased thirst and dehydration."**

### Legal Status (No Approved Medical Use)

**"it is not considered to be safe or effective in the treatment, diagnosis, prevention, or cure of any disease, and has no approved medical use"**

**"Possession and distribution of methcathinone for the purpose of human consumption is illegal under any/all circumstances in the United States and is either illegal or highly regulated in most jurisdictions worldwide."**
