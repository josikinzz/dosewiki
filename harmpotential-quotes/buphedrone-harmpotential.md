## Source: Wikipedia

### Thirst Suppression Danger

**It also causes a possibly dangerous effect of decreasing subjective feeling of thirst.**

### Limited Safety Data

**Though the specific effects and toxicity of buphedrone are not well studied**, in general it is expected to have overlapping effects with other synthetic cathinones.

### Legal High / Bath Salt

Like many other synthetic cathinones (e.g. mephedrone), **it is also found in so called "legal highs" (sometimes also called "bath salts"), a class of designer drugs used to bypass legal restrictions**.

### Scheduled

**In the United States, buphedrone is considered a schedule 1 controlled substance** as a positional isomer of mephedrone.
