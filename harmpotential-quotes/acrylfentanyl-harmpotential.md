## Source: Wikipedia

### More Potent Than Fentanyl

Acrylfentanyl (also known as acryloylfentanyl) is a **highly potent opioid analgesic** that is an analog of fentanyl and has been sold online as a designer drug. In animal studies the IC50 is 1.4 nM, being **slightly more potent than fentanyl itself** (1.6 nM) as well as having a **longer duration of action**.

### Life-Threatening Respiratory Depression

Side effects of fentanyl analogs are similar to those of fentanyl itself, which include itching, nausea and **potentially serious respiratory depression, which can be life-threatening**.

### Hundreds of Deaths from Fentanyl Analogs

**Fentanyl analogs have killed hundreds of people throughout Europe and the former Soviet republics** since the most recent resurgence in use began in Estonia in the early 2000s, and novel derivatives continue to appear.

### Potentially Naloxone Resistant

As acrylamide derivatives are often used in drug discovery to make covalent inhibitors which will bind irreversibly to its target, **acrylfentanyl is claimed to be naloxone resistant**.

### 22 Deaths Linked

**Acrylfentanyl has been linked to 20 deaths in Sweden as well as two deaths in Denmark in summer 2016.**
