## Dosage & Duration

### Oral

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 0.2 - 0.5 mg
- Light: 0.5 - 1 mg
- Common: 1 - 3 mg
- Strong: 3 - 4 mg
- Heavy: 4 - 6 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 4 - 8 hours
- Onset: 20 - 30 minutes
- After effects: 2 - 24 hours

Flunitrazepam ... is approximately 10 times more potent by weight than diazepam.
