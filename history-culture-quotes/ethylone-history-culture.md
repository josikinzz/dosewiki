## Source: PsychonautWiki

**3,4-methylenedioxy- *N* -ethylcathinone** (also known as **Ethylone** , **MDEC** and **βk-MDEA** ) is a synthetic [entactogen](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Entactogen) and [stimulant](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Stimulant) of the [cathinone](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Substituted_cathinone) class. It is the β-keto analog of [MDEA](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MDEA) ("Eve").

As a [designer drug](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Designer_drug) , ethylone is commonly sold on the street along with other [cathinones](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Substituted_cathinone) like [butylone](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Butylone) or [3-MMC](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/3-MMC) as a substitute or counterfeit for [MDMA](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MDMA) and/or [methylone](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Methylone) due to methylone's declining availability on the [research chemicals](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Research_chemical) market.

Ethylone has only a short history of human use and is reported to be less potent than its relative [methylone](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Methylone) as well as possessing more classical stimulant-type as opposed to entactogenic effects.

The toxicity and long-term health effects of recreational ethylone use do not seem to have been studied in any scientific context and the exact toxic dosage is unknown. This is because methylone has very little history of human usage.

## Source: Wikipedia

Ethylone, also known as 3,4-methylenedioxy-N-ethylcathinone (MDEC, βk-MDEA), is a recreational designer drug of the phenethylamine, amphetamine, and cathinone chemical classes. It is the β-keto analogue of MDEA ("Eve"). Ethylone has only a short history of human use and is reported to be less potent than its relative methylone. In the United States, it began to be found in cathinone products in late 2011.
Very little data exists about the pharmacological properties, metabolism, and toxicity of ethylone, and although several ethylone-related deaths have been reported, the cause of death was not due to ingestion of ethylone.
