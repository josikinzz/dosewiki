### Pharmacology

The mechanism of action that produces 2C-T's [hallucinogenic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Hallucinogenic) and [entheogenic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Entheogen) effects has not been established in scientific literature; however, its primary psychedelic effects are more than likely a result of its efficacy at the [5-HT2A receptor](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) as a [partial agonist](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Agonist#Agonists) . This mechanism of action is shared by many other psychedelic [phenethylamines](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Phenethylamine) and [tryptamines](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Tryptamine) .

However, the role of these interactions and how they result in the psychedelic experience continues to remain elusive.

Being similar in structure to [2C-T-2](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/2C-T-2) and [2C-T-7](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/2C-T-7) , this compound may have some [MAOI](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MAOI) properties.

## Source: Wikipedia

### Pharmacodynamics

2C-T shows affinity for the serotonin 5-HT2 receptors and is known to act as an agonist of the serotonin 5-HT2A and 5-HT2B receptors. The drug shows no affinity for the monoamine transporters.
