## Source: PsychonautWiki

5-Methoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine (also known as **5-MeO-DMT** ) is a [naturally-occurring](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Naturally-occurring) [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) substance of the [tryptamine](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Tryptamine) class. ... 5-MeO-DMT produces its effects by binding to [serotonin](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin) [receptors](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Receptors) in the brain, although the precise mechanism is not known.

5-MeO-DMT's psychedelic effects are best compared to [DMT](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/DMT) in terms of intensity (extreme) and duration (very short). However, it generally lacks a [visual geometry](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Geometry) component and tends to produce more [physical euphoria](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Physical_euphoria) as well as even stronger [transpersonal](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Transpersonal) or mystical-like effects (e.g. [ego loss](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Ego_loss) ) than DMT.

Unlike DMT, which has been shown to possess minimal physiological toxicity, 5-MeO-DMT's safety profile is much less established. A small number of deaths have been linked to its use and appear to be a result of physical overdose. [*[citation needed](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Citation_needed)*] Some reports suggest it may cause serotonin toxicity when combined with certain substances such as [MAOIs](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MAOIs) ; it may also have cardiotoxic properties. [*[citation needed](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Citation_needed)*]

### Pharmacology

5-Meo-DMT's [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) effects are primarily believed to come from its efficacy at the [5-HT1A receptor](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) as a [partial agonist](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Agonist#Agonists) . Specifically, this molecule shows a high binding affinity for the 5-HT 1A subtype and relatively low binding for the 5-HT 2a subtype. Since common psychedelics are thought to primarily elicit their effects through 5-HT 2a receptor activity, the drug’s binding profile and how it results in the [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) experience continues to remain elusive.

Additional mechanisms of action such as [reuptake inhibition](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Reuptake_inhibition) of [neurotransmitters](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Neurotransmitters) such as [serotonin](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin) , [noradrenaline](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Noradrenaline) and [dopamine](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Dopamine) are also thought to be involved to an extent. This can result in 5-MeO-DMT becoming dangerously toxic when combined with [MAOIs](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MAOIs) , [RIMAs](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/RIMA) , [SSRIs](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/SSRI) , [stimulants](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Stimulant) or any substance which acts as a [releasing agent](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Releasing_agent) or [reuptake inhibitor](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Reuptake_inhibitor) of [monoamine neurotransmitters](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Monoamine_neurotransmitter) .

5-MeO-DMT is *O* -demethylated by polymorphic cytochrome P450 2D6 (CYP2D6) to an active metabolite, [bufotenin](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Bufotenin) .

### Pharmacology

#### Pharmacodynamics

5-MeO-DMT is a methoxylated derivative of dimethyltryptamine (DMT). While most common psychedelics are believed to primarily elicit psychological effects through agonism of serotonin 5-HT2A receptors, 5-MeO-DMT shows 1,000-fold greater affinity for the serotonin 5-HT1A receptor over the serotonin 5-HT2A receptor. In line with its affinity for serotonin 5-HT1A receptors, 5-MeO-DMT is extremely potent at suppressing the firing of dorsal raphe nucleus serotonin neurons.

Similarly to other serotonergic psychedelics, 5-MeO-DMT is a non-selective serotonin receptor agonist, including of the serotonin 5-HT1A, 5-HT2A, 5-HT2B, and 5-HT2C receptors, among others. ... The drug is 4- to 10-fold more potent as a hallucinogen than DMT in humans. ...

Besides the serotonin receptors, 5-MeO-DMT is an agonist of the melatonin MT1 and MT2 receptors. Unlike DMT, 5-MeO-DMT is not a ligand or agonist of the sigma receptors. In contrast to certain other tryptamines, 5-MeO-DMT is inactive as a monoamine releasing agent, including of serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine. However, it is a weak serotonin reuptake inhibitor, with an IC50Tooltip half-maximal inhibitory concentration value of 2,184 nM.

#### Pharmacokinetics

5-MeO-DMT is lipophilic and is thought to easily cross the blood–brain barrier.

Bufotenin is an active metabolite of 5-MeO-DMT, formed by O-demethylation by cytochrome P450 CYP2D6. ... The metabolism of 5-MeO-DMT can be dramatically reduced and its levels markedly augmented and prolonged by monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs). In addition, MAOIs allow 5-MeO-DMT to become orally active in humans. ...

The elimination half-life of 5-MeO-DMT, administered sublingually, was found to be 28 minutes.
