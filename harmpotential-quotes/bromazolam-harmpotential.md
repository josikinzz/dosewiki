## Source: Wikipedia

### Limited Research

Because bromazolam is relatively new, **epidemiological research regarding the individual's health effects as well as the effects on the public health are quite scarce**. Most of the knowledge about the effects are known from case studies and individual reports.

### Tolerance, Addiction and Dependence

Similarly to prescription benzodiazepines, **bromazolam usage can lead to tolerance, addiction and dependence**.

### CNS Depression with Combinations

**Benzodiazepine-like compounds are rarely fatal when taken alone but can cause central nervous system depression when combined with other medication or drugs.**

### Common Adverse Effects

Common adverse effects of benzodiazepines are **somnolence, impaired balance, ataxia, loss of coordination, impaired thinking and self-assessment capability, muscle weakness, confusion, slurred speech, blurred vision, amnesia, dizziness, drowsiness, lethargy, fatigue and palpitations**.

### High Dose Effects

**At high doses, they may induce delirium, auditory and visual hallucinations, seizures, deep sleep and coma.**

### Addictive Nature Mechanism

**The sedative, anterograde amnesic, anticonvulsant actions, and the addictive nature of benzodiazepines, are due to the binding to the α1 subunit** of the (GABA)A receptor.

### Surging in US with Fentanyl

In June 2022, the U.S. Department of Justice reported that **bromazolam seizures were "surging" across the United States, driven in part by increasing detections alongside fentanyl**.

### Mixed with Opioids ("Benzo-Dope")

In Canada, **illicit opioids such as heroin or fentanyl analogues are mixed with bromazolam and sold at the street level; the product is sometimes referred to as "benzo-dope."**

### Counterfeit Xanax Arrests

Despite being unscheduled there have been **several arrests in the United States for mismarketing bromazolam as alprazolam, typically in the shape of brand name Xanax tablets** which constitutes a counterfeit drug charge among others.

### Upcoming Scheduling

In the United States, bromazolam is unscheduled at the federal level, however, pursuant to a notice made by the Drug Enforcement Administration, **on January 14, 2026, bromazolam will be placed into Schedule 1 federally** for 2 years with a possible extension for another year.

### UK Status

In the United Kingdom, **bromazolam is a Class C controlled substance**.
