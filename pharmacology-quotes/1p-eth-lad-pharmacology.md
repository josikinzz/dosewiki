## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Pharmacology

This compound likely acts as a [5-HT2A](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) [partial agonist](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Agonist#Agonists) . The [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) effects are believed to come from 1P-ETH-LAD's efficacy at the 5-HT 2A receptors. However, the role of these interactions and how they result in the psychedelic experience continues to remain elusive.

1P-ETH-LAD shares many common traits with its parent compound [LSD](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/LSD) ; it appears to be roughly equal in potency as well as similar in mechanism although the progression and duration of effects are compressed (while remaining qualitatively less intense and more manageable) due to suspected differences in how it is metabolized. Research has shown formation of [ETH-LAD](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/ETH-LAD) from 1P-ETH-LAD incubated in human serum, suggesting that it functions as a pro-drug.
