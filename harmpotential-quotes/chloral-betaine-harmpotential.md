### Classification

**habit-forming**, depressant

*[Note: Chloral betaine is a prodrug that metabolizes to chloral hydrate and then to trichloroethanol. The source materials contain limited harm information, but as a chloral hydrate prodrug, the following general risks apply:

- **Habit-forming** potential with physical and psychological dependence
- **CNS depression** risks including respiratory depression
- **Dangerous combinations** with other CNS depressants (alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines)
- **Overdose potential** - chloral hydrate has a narrow therapeutic index
- **Gastric irritation** - chloral hydrate is known to cause GI upset
- Historical use in "Mickey Finn" drugging incidents

Chloral hydrate overdoses have been fatal; the same risks would apply to chloral betaine.]*
