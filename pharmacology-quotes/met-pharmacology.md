## Source: Wikipedia

The drug acts as an agonist of the serotonin 5-HT2 receptors and to a lesser extent as a serotonin releasing agent.

MET is a serotonin 5-HT2A and 5-HT2C receptor partial agonist. It shows very weak activity as an agonist of the serotonin 5-HT1A and 5-HT2B receptors. In addition to acting at the serotonin 5-HT2 receptors, MET is a serotonin releasing agent with lower potency. It produces the head-twitch response, a behavioral proxy of psychedelic effects, in animals.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

As with most psychedelic tryptamines, MET is thought to act principally as a 5-HT2A partial agonist. The psychedelic effects are believed to come from MET's binding efficacy at the 5-HT2A receptors. However, the role of these interactions and how they result in the psychedelic experience continue to remain elusive.
