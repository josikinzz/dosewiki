## Source: DrugBank

### Absorption
Maximum concentration is generally reached in 1-1.5 hours. Taking dexmethylphenidate with or without food does not affect patients in a clinically relevant way.

### Half-life
The mean terminal half life is approximately 2.2 hours. However other studies have shown 3.8-3.9 hours, or 5.96 hours after intravenous administration and 5.69 hours following an oral dose.
