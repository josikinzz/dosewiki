## Source: Wikipedia

3-Fluoroamphetamine (3-FA; PAL-353) is a stimulant drug from the amphetamine family... It is self-administered by mice to a similar extent to related drugs such as 4-fluoroamphetamine and 3-methylamphetamine.

3-Fluoroamphetamine is distinguished by its potent stimulant and mild entactogenic effects, setting it apart from other amphetamines such as 4-FA, 2-FA, and 2-FMA. It lacks the productivity and focus-enhancing properties reported for 2-FA and 2-FMA, potentially limiting its appeal and availability.
