## Source: DrugBank

### Background

Naproxen is classified as a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory dug (NSAID) and was initially approved for prescription use in 1976 and then for over-the-counter (OTC) use in 1994.

### Toxicity

Although the over-the-counter (OTC) availability of naproxen provides convenience to patients, it also increases the likelihood of overdose.
