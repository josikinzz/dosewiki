### Fatal Doses

**"Ephylone may be fatal at heavy doses."**

**"Ephylone has been linked to numerous overdoses and deaths."**

### Death Statistics

**"The DEA claimed approximately 151 ephylone-related deaths occurred in the U.S. between 2014 and 2018."**

### Unknown Toxicity

**"Ephylone is a research chemical with a very short history of human usage. Very little is known about its long-term health effects and the exact toxic dosage is unknown."**

**"Numerous reports of hospitalizations and overdose deaths indicate that ephylone is extremely toxic at very high dosages."**

### Life-Threatening Symptoms

**"Higher dosages of ephylone have been linked to serotonin syndrome, rhabdomyolysis, kidney injury, acidemia and other life-threatening symptoms."**

### Adverse Effects Warning

**"Adverse effects become increasingly likely with higher doses and may include addiction, severe injury, or death."**

### Addiction Potential

**"As with other stimulants, the chronic use of ephylone can be considered moderately addictive with a high potential for abuse. It is capable of causing psychological dependence among certain users."**

**"When addiction has developed, cravings and withdrawal effects will occur if one suddenly stops their use."**

### Dangerous Interactions

**"Stimulants - dangerous increases in blood pressure and heart rate"**

**"25x-NBOMe/25x-NBOH - risk of excessive stimulation and heart strain...increased blood pressure, vasoconstriction, panic attacks, thought loops, seizures, and heart failure in extreme cases"**

**"Alcohol - risk of accidental over-intoxication...blackouts and severe respiratory depression"**

**"DXM - increased risk of panic attacks and hypertensive crisis, or serotonin syndrome"**

**"MDMA - neurotoxic effects...increased when other stimulants are present. There is also a risk of excessive blood pressure and heart strain (cardiotoxicity)."**

**"MXE - dangerously increase blood pressure and increase the risk of mania and psychosis"**

**"Dissociatives - risk of delusions, mania and psychosis...may be multiplied when combined"**

**"Tramadol - combinations with stimulants may further increase this risk [seizure threshold]"**
