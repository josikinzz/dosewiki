## Source: DrugBank

### Overview
Should only be used for acute treatment periods up to two or three weeks. Not recommended in pediatric patients less than 16 years of age.

### Absorption
The mean time to peak plasma concentrations (Tmax) of this drug was about 1.5-2 hours in clinical studies. Co-administration of a fatty meal with carisoprodol (350 mg tablet) had no impact on carisoprodol pharmacokinetics.

### Half-life
The terminal half-life is approximately 2 hours. The half-life of meprobamate is approximately 10 hours.

## Source: The Drug Classroom

### Dose
#### Oral (medical)
It’s sold as 250 and 350 mg tablets.
Common: 350 mg (3 – 4x per day)

#### Oral
Light: 100 – 350 mg
Common: 350 – 700 mg
Strong: 700+ mg

Those who take over 1400 mg (sometimes much more) more reliably report what some have described as a hypomanic state. It’s characterized by cheerfulness, disinhibition, socialization, physical euphoria, and sometimes psychomotor excitement (with impairment).
