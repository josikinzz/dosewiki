### Mass Overdose Event (100+ People)

On August 15, 2018, **70 people within the city of New Haven, Connecticut, started overdosing** near Yale University campus. By the end of the week, **the total number of overdosed had risen to over 100 people needing transport to local emergency rooms**. Three men were arrested, charged as drug dealers selling synthetic cannabis which contained AB-FUBINACA. First responders concluded that the overdoses were caused by opiate adulterants, as victims responded to Narcan. Almost all of the overdoses occurred on the New Haven Green, a large downtown park that is heavily traveled and very popular with the homeless population. There have been no deaths associated with these overdoses; however, **several victims were in critical or life-threatening condition** for a period of time.
