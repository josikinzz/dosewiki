## Source: DrugBank

### Absorption

The liquid formulations of hydrocodone have a Tmax of 0.83-1.33 h. The extended release tablet formulations have a Tmax of 14-16 h. The Cmax remains dose proportional over the range of 2.5-10 mg in liquid formulations and 20-120 mg in extended release formulations.

### Half-life

The half-life of elimination reported for hydrocodone is 7-9 h.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Dosage & Duration

### Oral

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 3 mg
- Light: 5 - 10 mg
- Common: 10 - 25 mg
- Strong: 25 - 40 mg
- Heavy: 40 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 4 - 8 hours
- Onset: 10 - 60 minutes

Tolerance to many of the effects of hydrocodone develops with prolonged use, including therapeutic effects. ... After cessation, it takes about 3 - 7 days for the tolerance to be reduced to half and 1 - 2 weeks to be back at baseline.

## Source: Wikipedia

Some references state that the analgesic action of hydrocodone begins in 20–30 minutes and lasts about 4–8 hours. The manufacturer's information says onset of action is about 10–30 minutes and duration is about 4–6 hours. Recommended dosing interval is 4–6 hours. Hydrocodone reaches peak serum levels after 1.3 hours.

Hydrocodone 10 mg is equivalent to about 10 mg of morphine by mouth.
