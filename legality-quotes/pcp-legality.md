## Source: Wikipedia

### History Section - Regulatory Context

> "Its use in humans was disallowed in the US in 1965 due to the high rates of side effects, while its use in animals was disallowed in 1978."

> "PCP is classified as a schedule II drug in the US."

### Regulation Section (Dedicated)

> "PCP is a Schedule II substance in the US. The Administrative Controlled Substances Code Number (ACSCN) for PCP is 7471. Its manufacturing quota for 2014 was 19 grams (0.67 oz). It is a Schedule I drug by the Controlled Drugs and Substances act in Canada, a List I drug of the Opium Law in the Netherlands, and a Class A substance in the UK."

### Analogues Section - Federal Analog Act Coverage

> "In the United States, all of these compounds would be considered controlled substance analogs of PCP under the Federal Analog Act and are hence illegal drugs if sold for human consumption."
