## Source: Isomer Design (TiHKAL)

### Toxic Component at Higher Doses

**"At modest dosages in the 70–80 mg area orally, Indapex is both relaxing and sexually stimulating. The highest dosages studied seem to reveal a toxic component and few subjects chose to repeat these levels."**

### High Dose Report - Emotional Disturbance

**"(with 150 mg, orally) At seventy minutes mild nausea and gastric fullness was apparent. There was a loss of fine motor skills and I found it hard to walk. My peripheral visual field has a waviness to it. Both television and a radio talk show became completely uninteresting and difficult to follow, and a feeling of sadness and despair became overwhelming. Emotions were extremely labile, going from profound crying to calm and back in a period of twenty minutes. I saw every defect and failure in my life."**

### High Dose Report - Physical Distress

**"Apparent body temperature fluctuations were continuous, going from being hot to chilled over and over again. It was impossible to get comfortable for several hours. The stomach distress continued throughout the entire experience, with a moderately severe stomach aching during the third and fourth hours."**

### Dehydration

**"No eating, or even drinking of water, was possible until the eighth hour, at which time small sips of water were tolerated. I awoke several times during the night and took in additional small amounts of water, but still arose the next morning very dehydrated."**
