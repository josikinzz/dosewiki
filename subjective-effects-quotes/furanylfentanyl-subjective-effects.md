## Source: Wikipedia

Furanylfentanyl (Fu-F) is an opioid analgesic that is an analog of fentanyl... Research done in the 1980's showed that in humans furanylfentanyl is 50-100 times more potent than morphine, making it roughly half as potent as fentanyl...

Side effects of fentanyl analogs are similar to those of fentanyl itself, which include itching, nausea and potentially serious respiratory depression, which can be life-threatening.
