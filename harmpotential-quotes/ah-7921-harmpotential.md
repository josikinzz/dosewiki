## Source: The Drug Classroom

### Over a Dozen Deaths

**It has been detected in over a dozen deaths**, though the majority of those cases involved other drugs, often CNS depressants.

### Respiratory Depression

**Respiratory depression is a concern when too much is taken.**

For respiratory depression, it has been listed as both more and less potent than morphine. One report claimed it was **1.6x more potent for respiratory depression, meaning the negative effects may become an issue pretty close to the effective analgesic dose**.

### Swedish Deaths

Between December 2012 and March 2013, **Swedish authorities reported five non-fatal intoxications**. From January 2013 to September 2013, those cases were followed by **10 deaths in Sweden involving AH-7921**. Other drugs were detected in the fatalities, though **AH-7921 was listed as the cause in two cases**.

### Case Reports

**Case 1:** A 19-year-old male was found dead in bed by a friend. **AH-7921 was presumed to be a major factor**. Heart blood: 3.9 mg/L. Peripheral blood: 9.1 mg/L.

**Case 7:** A young female was found dead at home by her boyfriend. Small bags labeled "AH-7921" and "Etizolam" were found at the scene.

### Unknown Safe Dose

**Not enough information exists to say what a reliably low-risk dose will be.**

### Risky Combinations

**Using any other CNS depressants (e.g. benzodiazepines, opioids, alcohol) will make it easier to develop respiratory depression**, among other things.

## Source: Wikipedia

### Effects Similar to Heroin/Fentanyl

With doses that usually range from 10 to 150 mg, users are likely to experience **effects similar to heroin, morphine, and fentanyl such as euphoria and respiratory depression**.

### Overdose Effects

**When an overdose occurs users often experience tachycardia, hypertension, and seizures.**

### Steep Dose Response Curve

Mice, dogs, and monkeys, have been used in tests which showed the drug was almost equivalently potent to morphine, and **had a very steep dose response curve**.

### Withdrawal Symptoms

Rats given 20 mg doses three times a day for five days experienced **withdrawal symptoms similar to other opioids**. Reports have shown users to experience **depression and insomnia when withdrawing from this drug**.

### 13 of 14 Cases Resulted in Death

**A 2018 review of published case reports found a total of 14 cases, of which 13 resulted in death.**

### Multi-Country Fatalities

**Within ten months from 2013 to 2014, there had been serious fatalities reported from this drug from four different countries.** The first fatality was a 19-year-old male who had a 3.9 mg/L AH-7921 concentration of heart blood leading medical examiners to **confirm the death was an opioid intoxication**.

### Deaths at Varying Concentrations

Overall, **fatalities occurred with low and high concentrations of AH-7921 in femoral blood**.

### Legal Status

- **Australia** - Schedule 9 prohibited substance (May 2014)
- **UK** - Class A drug (January 2015)
- **United States** - Schedule I (May 2016)
- **Canada** - Schedule I (2016) - Possession can result in **maximum 7 years imprisonment**
