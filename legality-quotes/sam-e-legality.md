## Source: DrugBank

### Indication

S-Adenosylmethionine (SAMe) is used as a drug in Europe for the treatment of depression, liver disorders, fibromyalgia, and osteoarthritis. It has also been introduced into the United States market as a dietary supplement for the support of bone and joint health, as well as mood and emotional well being.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

SAM-e is available over the counter and by prescription in the treatment of depression and osteoarthritis.

## Legal status

- **United States** - SAM-e is approved and legal over the counter for purchase.
- **Germany** - SAM-e is sold as a dietary supplement.
- **Russia** - SAM-e is available by prescription.
- **United Kingdom** - It is illegal to produce, supply, or import this drug under the Psychoactive Substance Act, which came into effect on May 26th, 2016.
