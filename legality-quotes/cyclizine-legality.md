## Source: Wikipedia

It is on the World Health Organization's List of Essential Medicines. In the United States it is available over the counter.

The drug Diconal is a combination of cyclizine and the opioid dipipanone. Dipipanone is a schedule I controlled substance in the US.

It is an over-the-counter drug in many countries because it has been well tolerated, although it has not been studied much.

It has been used illegally in greyhound racing to sabotage a dog's performance.
