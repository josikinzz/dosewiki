## Source: Wikipedia

Gabapentin acts by... [reducing] the release of excitatory neurotransmitters (primarily glutamate) and as a result, reduces excess excitation of neuronal networks in the spinal cord and brain. Sleepiness and dizziness are the most common side effects.

Gabapentin is effective for the long-term treatment of social anxiety disorder and in reducing preoperative anxiety. Gabapentin enhances slow-wave sleep in people with primary insomnia. It also improves sleep quality by elevating sleep efficiency and decreasing spontaneous arousal.

Dizziness and somnolence are the most frequent side effects. Fatigue, ataxia, peripheral edema (swelling of extremities), and nystagmus are also common. ... dificuldades in mentation and visual disturbances as compared to a placebo. ... Case studies indicate that it may cause anorgasmia and erectile dysfunction...

When taken in excess, gabapentin can induce euphoria, a sense of calm, improved sociability, and reduced alcohol or cocaine cravings.
