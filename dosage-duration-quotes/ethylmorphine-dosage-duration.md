## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Dosage & Duration

### Oral

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 20 mg
- Light: 40 - 50 mg
- Common: 50 - 100 mg
- Strong: 100 - 200 mg
- Heavy: 200 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 4 - 5 hours
- Onset: 15 - 30 minutes
- Peak: 2 - 3 hours

Ethylmorphine has been described as less potent than morphine but slightly more potent than codeine.

Tolerance to many of the effects of ethylmorphine develops with prolonged and repeated use. ... After cessation, it takes about 3 - 7 days for the tolerance to be reduced to half and 1 - 2 weeks to be back at baseline.
