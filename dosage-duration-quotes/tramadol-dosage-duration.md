## Source: DrugBank

### Background
Tramadol is a centrally acting synthetic opioid analgesic and SNRI ... structurally related to codeine and morphine. ... It has about 1/10th of the potency of morphine.

### Absorption
Following administration, racemic tramadol is rapidly and almost completely absorbed, with a bioavailability of 75%. ... Peak plasma concentrations of tramadol and the primary metabolite M1 occur at two and three hours, respectively. ... Following intramuscular administration [of 50mg] ... Tmax of 0.75 hours. ... Following rectal administration [of 100mg] ... Tmax of 3.3 hours.

### Half-life
Tramadol reported a half-life of 5-6 hours while the M1 metabolite presents a half-life of 8 hours.
