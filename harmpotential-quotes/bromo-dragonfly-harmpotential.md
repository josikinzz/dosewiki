## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Multiple Hospitalizations and Deaths

*Bromo-DragonFLY is an extremely potent and rare hallucinogenic substance* — **Bromo-DragonFLY has been linked to an unusually large number of hospitalizations and deaths, as such it is a strongly advised against using this substance.**

*It is also worth noting that these effects will not necessarily occur in a predictable or reliable manner, although higher doses are more liable to induce the full spectrum of effects. Likewise, **adverse effects** become increasingly likely with higher doses and may include **addiction, severe injury, or death***

### Extreme Potency Warning

**Bromo-DragonFLY is extremely potent and is active in the microgram range (µg), requiring specialized equipment to measure accurately and safely; ingesting too much of this substance may result in hospitalization or death.**

### Extremely Toxic

**Bromo-dragonfly is reported to be extremely toxic at very high dosages. Symptoms of toxicity include: hyperthermia, vasoconstriction, tachycardia, hypertension, seizures, and death.**

### Physical Effects - Vasoconstrictive Tissue Damage

**The abnormal heartbeat and appearance of tachycardia is likely caused by the drug having 5-HT2B activation properties which could lead to development of cardiac conditions or disorders. Severe injury and death have been reported at higher doses.**

**Vasoconstriction** - This compound is notable for having potent vasoconstrictive effects, **which can result in severe tissue damage or even limb amputation in extreme cases**.

### Necrosis from Overdose

**Necrosis** - When overdosed, bromo-dragonfly can lead to severe vasoconstriction and, consequently, necrosis.

### Fatal Overdose Warning

**Fatal overdose may occur when psychedelics with stimulating properties like bromo-dragonfly are combined with other stimulants.**

### Dangerous Interactions

- **Stimulants** - Bromo-DragonFly can be unpredictable in its interactions with other substances, particularly stimulants. Due to this unpredictability, **it is best to approach such combinations with extreme caution. Combining stimulants and bromo-dragonfly could increase the risk of adverse effects such as anxiety, tachycardia, hypertension, vascoconstriction, necrosis, and, in extreme cases, heart failure.**
- **Tramadol** - Tramadol is well documented to lower the seizure threshold and psychedelics also have the potential to trigger seizures in susceptible individuals. **There have been many anecdotal reports of seizures occurring from this combination.**
- **Lithium** - Lithium is commonly prescribed in the treatment of bipolar disorder. **There is a large body of anecdotal evidence that suggests taking it with psychedelics significantly increases the risk of psychosis and seizures.**
- **Cannabis** - Cannabis has an unexpectedly strong and somewhat unpredictable synergy with psychedelics. **Extreme caution is advised when using this combination as it can significantly increase the risk of adverse psychological effects like anxiety, paranoia, panic attacks, and psychosis.**

### Tolerance Cross-Reference

**Cross tolerances have been reported between 2C-B-FLY (a less potent compound) and Bromo-DragonFLY. Users should always be aware of this, as 2C-B-FLY is considerably less potent than Bromo-DragonFLY, which means that if they develop cross tolerance from 2C-B-FLY and take Bromo-DragonFLY without compensating for cross-tolerance they may consume more than needed.**

## Source: Wikipedia

### At Least Five Deaths

**There have been at least five deaths believed to have resulted from Bromo-DragonFLY.**

### Amputation Case - Vasoconstriction

**In October 2009, a man in Haugesund, Norway injected himself with Bromo-DragonFLY and later had to have the front part of his feet and several fingers on one hand amputated, besides several plastic surgery operations on his hands, due to vasoconstriction and the resultant tissue damage.**

### Death at 700μg

**In 2012, a 19-year-old in the United States died at a hospital after ingesting an unknown amount, later confirmed to be 700μg, of bromo-dragonfly.**

### Multiple International Deaths

Deaths in **Denmark, Sweden and Finland** have been reported as well.

### Vomiting Blood, Terrifying Hallucinations

Side effects include **vomiting blood, and terrifying hallucinations**.

### Mislabeled Batch Deaths

**Two deaths occurred following a drug dealer mistakenly selling Bromo-DragonFLY in powder form as 2C-B-FLY. In powder form, this mistake is easy to make as both are white powders. 2C-B-FLY has around one twentieth the potency** - a dealer who confused the two and sold the more potent drug could easily cause users to overdose.

### Duration of Days

**Duration: The psychoactive effects are reported to last 1 to 3 days. An extreme duration of 6 days has also been reported.**
