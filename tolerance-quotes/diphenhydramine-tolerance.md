## Source: Disregard Everything I Say

**Physical health effects, potential addiction and tolerance:**

If taken on any kind of a regular basis, DPH can have some serious but unstudied adverse effects on ones kidneys and result in bladder issues similar to that of ketamine cystitis.

It is assumed non-addictive by most due to the nature of the experience but many people do find themselves coming back to it on a regular basis. This can often lead to addiction and extreme caution should be taken.

Diphenhydramine can become fatal if amounts close to or exceeding 2 grams are consumed and can result in death when combined with most stimulants and MAOI inhibitors. It is worth noting however that a dosage of 1 gram is considered extreme in its strength and could potentially result in a fatal overdose if a person happened to be particularly sensitive to this substance.

In terms of its tolerance, this forms very quickly with DPH trips, meaning that a trip can only occur at full effects every 2-4 weeks.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Dependence and abuse potential
Diphenhydramine produces dependence with chronic use. In comparison to other [hallucinogens](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Hallucinogens) , DPH has been reported to have significantly less abuse potential than other hallucinogens. Most people who try it do not wish to repeat the experience.

Tolerance to many of the effects of DPH develops with repeated use. Users have to administer increasingly large doses to achieve the impact. After that, it takes about 1 - 2 weeks for tolerance to return to baseline (in the absence of further consumption). DPH presents cross-tolerance with all [deliriants](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Deliriant) , meaning that after the consumption of DPH, all [deliriants](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Deliriant) will have a reduced effect.
