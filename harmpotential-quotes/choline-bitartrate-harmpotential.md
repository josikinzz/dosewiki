## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Adverse Effects Warning

*Likewise, **adverse effects** become increasingly likely with higher doses and may include **addiction, severe injury, or death*** ☠.

### Side Effects

- **Headaches**
- **Body odor alteration** - This occurs in some populations, especially those suffering from trimethylaminuria. Choline is a precursor to trimethylamine, which some persons are not able to easily break down, **often resulting in a "fishy smell."**

### Toxicity Assessment

As choline is a natural precursor to acetylcholine which is naturally found in the body, **it is well tolerated and therefore unlikely to be harmful in any way. Choline is non-addictive, is not known to cause brain damage, and has an extremely low toxicity relative to dose.**

## Source: Wikipedia

### Irritation Hazards

**May cause skin, eye and respiratory system irritation. May cause gastrointestinal system irritation as well.**

### High Dose Effects

**If swallowed in high doses, may cause dizziness, nausea, vomiting and diarrhea, and a rotten fish-like body smell resulting from the excretion of trimethylamine from the body** (trimethylamine is a choline metabolite).

### Depression Risk

**There are reports of depression or increased symptoms of it in patients using high doses of choline bitartrate.**

### LD50

Oral **LD50 value for a human is estimated to be 200 to 400 grams (as choline)**.

### Skin Absorption Warning

Nevertheless, **choline bitartrate can be harmful, if absorbed through skin**.
