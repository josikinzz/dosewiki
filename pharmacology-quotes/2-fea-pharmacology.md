### Pharmacology

2-FEA has not yet been formally studied to the same extent as traditional [amphetamines](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Amphetamines) . Currently, it is assumed that it most likely acts primarily as a triple [reuptake inhibitor](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Reuptake_inhibitor) , and that it releases the neurotransmitters: [serotonin](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin) , [dopamine](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Dopamine) , and [norepinephrine](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Norepinephrine) . 2-FEA likely creates it effects by acting as a releasing agent of said neurotransmitters and/or by binding to- and partially blocking the transporter proteins that normally clear substances from the [synaptic cleft,](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Synaptic_cleft) after they have fulfilled their function of conducting a neural impulse.

## Source: Wikipedia

Note: The Wikipedia entry appears to be for "2-Furylethylamine" rather than "2-Fluoroethamphetamine". The following information may refer to a different compound:

2-Furylethylamine (2-FEA or FEA) is a drug of the arylalkylamine family related to the substituted phenethylamines such as β-phenethylamine (PEA) and amphetamine. It is known to have similar pressor effects as amphetamine and strong constricting effects on the uterus in animals. The psychoactive effects of FEA in humans are unknown.
