- **Spontaneous tactile sensations**
- **Euphoria**
- **Stimulation**
- **Nausea**
- **Bodily control enhancement**
- **Tactile enhancement**
- **Temperature regulation loss**
- **Increased heart rate**
- **Pupil dilation**

- **Novelty enhancement**

- **Immersion enhancement**

- **Ego death**
- **Unity and interconnectedness**
- **Wakefulness**
- **Increased libido**
