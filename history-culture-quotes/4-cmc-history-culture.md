## Source: Wikipedia

4-Chloromethcathinone (also known as 4-CMC and clephedrone) is a stimulant drug of the cathinone class that has been sold online as a designer drug.
4-CMC produces similar effects to mephedrone, and has been sold as an alternative in countries where mephedrone was scheduled.

### Legality

- **Germany:** 4-CMC is an Anlage I controlled drug in Germany.
- **Sweden:** Sweden's public health agency suggested classifying 4-Chloromethcathinone (klefedron) as illegal narcotic on June 1, 2015.
- **China:** As of October 2015, 4-CMC is a controlled substance in China.
- **Virginia (USA):** 4-CMC is considered a Schedule 1 substance in Virginia.
- **UNODC:** In December 2019, the UNODC announced scheduling recommendations placing 4-CMC into Schedule II.
