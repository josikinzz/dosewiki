## Source: PsychonautWiki

4-Acetoxy-N-methyl-N-isopropyltryptamine (abbreviated **4-AcO-MiPT** and also known as **mipracetin** ) is a synthetic [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) [tryptamine](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Tryptamine) . It is the acetylated form of [4-HO-MiPT](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-HO-MiPT) (also known as miprocin) and is a higher homolog of [4-AcO-DMT](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-AcO-DMT) , [4-AcO-MET](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-AcO-MET) and [4-AcO-DET](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-AcO-DET) . These substances are commonly hypothesized to act primarily as prodrugs for their respective hydroxylated counterparts (e.g. [4-HO-DMT](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-HO-DMT) , [4-HO-MET](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-HO-MET) and [4-HO-DET](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-HO-DET) ), although there is on-going debate as to whether they possess their own innate activity. [*[citation needed](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Citation_needed)*]

There is very little information on the human pharmacology or toxicity of 4-AcO-MiPT, although analytical methods have been developed for its detection.

### Pharmacology

Like with most [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) tryptamines, 4-AcO-MiPT is thought to act principally as a [5-HT2A](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) [partial agonist](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Agonist#Agonists) . The psychedelic effects are believed to come from 4-AcO-MiPT's binding efficacy at the 5-HT 2A receptors.

However, the role of these interactions and how they result in the psychedelic experience continues to remain elusive.
