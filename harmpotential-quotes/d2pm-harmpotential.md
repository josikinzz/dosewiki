## Source: The Drug Classroom

### Overdose & Deaths

**Overdoses have led to hospital visits, but no deaths have been reported so far.**

**In overdose cases, stimulation and some psychological effects may persist for over 24 hours.**

### Adverse Effects & Warnings

**Not enough information exists to say what a reasonably safe dose is or how safe chronic use is.** To reduce the chance of harm, it should be taken at common doses, without combinations, and as infrequently as possible.

**The acute effects tend to decline in under 6 hours, but there are cases (likely overdoses) where users have experienced negative effects for over 24 hours.** Insomnia and anxiety are two primary symptoms that have been reported for up to 96 hours post-administration.

### Side Effects (Reported Negative Effects)

- **Anxiety**
- **Paranoia**
- **Insomnia**
- **Jaw clenching**
- **Hypertension**
- **Hyperthermia**
- **Tachycardia**

### Cellular Toxicity

**One study examined the in vitro differences between D2PM, MDMA, and MDA in terms of their cellular toxicity. In the study, which used PC12 cells, D2PM was more toxic and it inhibited the neurite outgrowth that would normally appear after NGF (nerve growth factor) exposure.**

IC50 values for toxicity:
- R-D2PM = 1.00 mM
- S-D2PM = 0.77 mM

### Case Reports - Adverse Outcomes

**Case 1:** 33-year-old male took eight capsules of "Benzofury" over 6 hours. **He arrived at the ED 48 hours after using the drug and exhibited anxiety and paranoia. Dilated pupils were observed.**

**Case 2:** 21-year-old male took one capsule of unknown stimulant. **Nausea, vomiting, and a racing heart occurred.** Those symptoms eventually dissipated, but **the user was left with insomnia and anxiety, which were still present upon reaching the ED 48 hours post-administration.**

**Case 3:** 29-year-old male used 1 gram of "NRG-3" and a small amount of MDMA powder. **He went to the ED 60 hours after use and complained of prolonged insomnia. His pupils were dilated.**

**Case 4:** 25-year-old male took an unknown liquid believed to contain GHB or GBL. After using the substance, **he collapsed. Anxiety and insomnia were then reported for 96 hours. Dilated pupils were observed.**

**Case 5:** 17-year-old male intranasally used 1 gram of "MDMA" and **developed tachycardia, paranoia, and visual hallucinations. 24 hours after taking the drug, he still had insomnia, paranoia, visual hallucinations, tachycardia (120), hypertension, and dilated pupils.**

**Case 6:** 21-year-old male took 3 tablets of "Head Candy." After also using poppers, **he developed ischemic-like chest pain. He entered the ED two hours after the chest pain subsided, though he still had tachycardia (124), hypertension, agitation, and pupil dilation.**

### Cardiovascular Toxicity

**Side effects including chest pain (suggestive of possible cardiovascular toxicity)** have been seen following recreational use of diphenylprolinol, although it was combined with glaucine in a party pill product, thus making it impossible to say for certain which drug was responsible.
