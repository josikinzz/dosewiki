## Source: DrugBank

Midazolam is a short-acting hypnotic-sedative drug with anxiolytic, muscle relaxant, anticonvulsant, sedative, hypnotic, and amnesic properties.

### Physical Effects
- **Sedation:** Midazolam is a short-acting benzodiazepine central nervous system (CNS) depressant. The onset of sedation after intramuscular administration in adults is 15 minutes, with maximal sedation occurring 30-60 minutes after injection. Sedation in both adult and pediatric patients is reached within 3 to 5 minutes post intravenous (IV) injection.
- **Muscle Relaxation:** Pharmacodynamic properties include muscle relaxant activities.
- **Motor Control:** Signs of overdose include impaired coordination and diminished reflexes.
- **Sleep:** Benzodiazepines increase the activity of GABA, thereby producing a sedating effect, relaxing skeletal muscles, and inducing sleep.
- **Toxicity/Overdose Sensations:** Signs of overdose include sedation, somnolence, and coma. Serious cardiorespiratory adverse reactions, including hypoventilation, airway obstruction, apnea, and hypotension have been reported.

### Cognitive Effects
- **Anxiolysis:** Used as an agent for anxiolysis (anxiety reduction).
- **Amnesia/Memory:** Midazolam has amnestic properties. In one study of adults, 73% of the patients who were administered midazolam intramuscularly had no recollection of memory cards shown 30 minutes following drug administration; 40% had no recollection of the memory cards shown 60 minutes after drug administration. Seventy-one (71%) percent of the adult patients in clinical endoscopy studies had no memory of insertion of the endoscope; 82% of the patients had no memory of withdrawal of the endoscope. Impairment in a memory test was observed in 90% of the patients during anesthetic induction.
- **Confusion:** Signs of overdose include confusion.

## Source: Safer Party

### Physical Effects
- **General:** Midazolam has a calming, relaxing, antispasmodic, and sleep-inducing effect.
- **Sedation:** Triggers sedative (calming) and hypnotic effects in the body.
- **Muscle Relaxation:** Produces muscle-relaxing effects.
- **Undesirable Side Effects:** Prolonged fatigue, gastrointestinal problems, impaired reactions, hypersensitivity reactions, headaches, dizziness, motor difficulties, visual disturbances, slowed breathing, muscle weakness, random movements, and speech and movement disorders.
- **Withdrawal Symptoms:** Physical weakness, tremors, sleep disturbances, headaches, sweating, and nausea.

### Cognitive Effects
- **Anxiolysis:** Anxiolytic (anxiety-relieving) effects.
- **Perception:** Feelings and perceptions are dampened.
- **Undesirable Side Effects:** Confusion, aggression, outbursts of anger, and restlessness.
- **Memory:** Long-term use can lead to memory disorders/loss and listlessness (hangover effects).
- **Withdrawal Symptoms:** Inner restlessness, hallucinations, and depression.
