## Source: The Drug Classroom

When compared to other dissociatives, PCP is more likely to produce psychosis. According to studies on the drug, agitation or hallucinations occurred in up to 50% of patients given anesthetic doses. And, it also resulted in greater mental issues in ~17% of those given anesthetic doses.

High doses have been associated with dangerous psychotic states. This isn’t a typical response, but it’s certainly possible, particularly when large amounts are used.

## Source: TripSit Factsheets

The drug is best known for stories of strange and sometimes violent behaviour of those under its influence, though it is likely these are only in overdose cases. Caution is recommended due to reports of mania and psychosis.

*Note: NOTE: Caution is strongly recommended, there are a high amount of reports for the drug inducing manic episodes and or related psychosis.*

### Interactions
* **amphetamines**: This combination can easily lead to hypermanic states
* **benzodiazepines**: Both substances potentiate the ataxia and sedation caused by the other and can lead to unexpected loss of consciousness at high doses. While unconscious, vomit aspiration is a risk if not placed in the recovery position. Memory blackouts are likely
* **cocaine**: This combination can easily lead to hypermanic states
* **mdma**: This combination can easily lead to hypermanic states
