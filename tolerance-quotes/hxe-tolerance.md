## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Tolerance and addiction potential

As with other nmda receptor antagonists, the chronic use of HXE can be considered moderately addictive with a high potential for abuse and is capable of producing psychological dependence among certain users. when addiction has developed, cravings and [withdrawal effects](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Withdrawal_effects) may occur if a person suddenly stops their usage.

tolerance to many of the effects of HXE develops with prolonged and repeated use. this results in users having to administer increasingly large doses to achieve the same effects. after that, it takes about 3 - 7 days for the tolerance to be reduced to half and 1 - 2 weeks to be back at baseline (in the absence of further consumption).

HXE presents cross-tolerance with all [dissociatives](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Dissociative) , meaning that after the consumption of HXE all [dissociatives](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Dissociative) will have a reduced effect.
