## Source: PsychonautWiki

### Oral
**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 0.5 mg
- Light: 0.5 - 1 mg
- Common: 1 - 3 mg
- Strong: 3 - 4 mg
- Heavy: 4 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 8 - 12 hours
- Onset: 10 - 45 minutes

In animal studies, it has a potency of approximately ten times that of diazepam.

## Source: Wikipedia

Diclazepam has an approximate elimination half-life of 42 hours and undergoes N-demethylation to delorazepam, which can be detected in urine for 6 days following administration... Other metabolites detected were lorazepam and lormetazepam which were detectable in urine for 19 and 11 days, respectively.
