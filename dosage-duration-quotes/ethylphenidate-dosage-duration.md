### Rectal

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: < 5 mg
- Light: 5 - 15 mg
- Common: 15 - 30 mg
- Strong: 30 - 50 mg
- Heavy: 50 mg+

**Duration:**
- Total: 2 - 6 hours
- Onset: 5 - 20 minutes

Tolerance to many of the effects of ethylphenidate develops with prolonged and repeated use. This results in users having to administer increasingly large doses to achieve the same effects. After that, it takes about 3 - 7 days for the tolerance to be reduced to half and 1 - 2 weeks to be back at baseline.
