## Source: Wikipedia

### Pharmacodynamics

25E-NBOMe acts as an agonist of the serotonin 5-HT2 receptors. In accordance with its psychedelic effects in humans, it produces the head-twitch response, a behavioral proxy of psychedelic effects, in rodents.
25E-NBOMe has shown reinforcing effects in rodents. This included conditioned place preference (CPP) and self-administration. The reinforcing effects of 25E-NBOMe were mediated by increased dopaminergic signaling in the nucleus accumbens. Blockade of the dopamine D1 receptor could attenuate the CPP induced by 25E-NBOMe.
