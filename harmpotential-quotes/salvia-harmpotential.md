### Toxicity & Safety

**"Although not thoroughly assessed, preliminary research indicates S. divinorum may have low toxicity (high LD50)."**

**"In experiments, salvinorin A has shown little toxicity. Rodents chronically exposed to levels many times greater than those to which humans expose themselves show no signs of organ damage."**

### Mental Health & Psychological Effects

**"Because the plant has not been well-studied in high-quality clinical research, little is known about its toxicology, adverse effects, or safety over long-term consumption."**

Brett said that he had ceased his experimentation, but his parents did not believe that he was telling the truth.
