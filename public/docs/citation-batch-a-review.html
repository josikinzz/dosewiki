## Guardrails — these matter more than throughput

1. **Never fabricate a citation.** If no defensible non-wiki source supports a claim, the
   correct outcome is `missing_preserve_original`, which maps to `needs_source` in the
   tracker. Leave the claim unmarked and record the gap. A section returning
   "no source found" is a **successful** run, not a failed one.
2. **Wikipedia and other wikis are not defensible sources.** The entire point of
   `needs_source` is to avoid laundering wiki content into citations.
3. **Do not write to Convex.** This workflow produces a draft for human review only. The
   adapter rejects Convex writes. There is no `--write` / `--confirm` escape hatch in this
   skill, and there should not be one.
4. **Stop at `needs_review`.** `finalize` fixes the run outcome to `needs_review` and
   freezes the manifest. That is the intended end state and the human handback point. Do
   not attempt to apply, promote, or queue the draft.
5. **Preserve original prose.** This is a marker-only workflow — it adds `[cite:…]`
   markers and references. It does not rewrite article text.
