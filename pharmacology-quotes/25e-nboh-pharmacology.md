### Pharmacology

25E-NBOH's [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelics) effects are believed to be mediated by its binding to the [5-HT2A](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) and [5-HT2C](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) [receptors](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Receptor) as an agonist. Information regarding the pharmacological properties of 25E-NBOH is still scarce, but its action is similar to that of the other [serotonergic psychedelics](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonergic_psychedelic) .

## Source: Wikipedia

25E-NBOH (2C-E-NBOH, NBOH-2C-E) is a derivative of the phenethylamine derived hallucinogen 2C-E. It was first developed by Martin Hansen at the University of Copenhagen in 2010 as a brain imaging agent, but has subsequently been sold as a designer drug, first being identified in Brazil in 2018 on seized blotter paper, as well as in Slovenia and France. It acts as a potent serotonin receptor agonist with similar affinity to better-known compounds such as 25I-NBOMe at 5-HT2A and 5-HT2C receptors.
