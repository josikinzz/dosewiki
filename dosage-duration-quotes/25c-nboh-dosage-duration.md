## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Dosage & Duration

### Sublingual

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 100 µg
- Light: 250 - 500 µg
- Common: 500 - 750 µg
- Strong: 750 - 1000 µg
- Heavy: 25C-NBOH may be fatal at heavy doses.

**Duration:**
- Total: 5 - 7 hours
- Onset: 15 - 45 minutes
- Come up: 30 - 90 minutes
- After effects: 3 - 6 days

"It is worth noting that compounds of the NBOH family are not orally active and should be administered sublingually by placing and holding it into one's mouth and allowing it to absorb over a period of 15-25 minutes."

"Anecdotal reports suggest that this substance may be difficult to use safely due to its highly sensitive dose-response and unpredictable effects."

"Assuming the substance has been taken sublingually, the very first physical effect which a person will notice immediately after sublingual absorption is a strong, unpleasant metallic chemical taste. This is accompanied by a very distinct feeling of general numbness of the tongue and mouth which can stay for up to an hour after the blotter paper has been consumed."

"25C-NBOH's extreme potency means it should not be insufflated (snorted) as this method of administration has been associated with many deaths and hospitalizations with closely related members of the 25x-NBOMe series."
