**U.S. FEDERAL LEGAL SUMMARY**
**3C-P**
- REGULATED: No
- STATUS: Not Approved ForHuman Consumption

3C-P is an uncontrolled substance in the U.S. as well as in most if not all other countries. It could conceivably be covered under the Analog Act depending on how it's sold, but there have been no cases prosecuted that we are aware of. (11/03/2004) If sold as a supplement, sales must conform to U.S. supplement laws. If sold for consumption as a food or drug, sales are regulated by the FDA.

## Source: PsychonautWiki

## Legal status

- **United Kingdom** : It is illegal to produce, supply, or import this drug under the Psychoactive Substance Act, which came into effect on May 26th, 2016.
- **United States** : 3C-P is technically not scheduled in the United States, but could be considered an analog of [mescaline](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Mescaline) and may, therefore, be considered a Schedule I drug under the Federal Analogue Act.
- **Japan** : 3C-P is a controlled substance in Japan effective February 28th, 2018.
