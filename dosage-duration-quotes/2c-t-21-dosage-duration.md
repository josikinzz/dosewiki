## Dosage & Duration

### Oral

**Dosage:**
- Threshold: 5 mg
- Light: 5 - 10 mg
- Common: 10 - 12 mg
- Strong: 12 - 15 mg
- Heavy: 15 mg +

**Duration:**
- Total: 10 - 12 hours
- Onset: 90 - 120 minutes
- Peak: 3 - 4 hours
- Offset: 2 - 3 hours
- After effects: 2 - 4 hours

"The dosage range is listed as 8–12 mg. It is described in various reports as being generally relaxed and clear-headed with mild visual effects."

"In March 2004, a 22-year-old American male died after ingesting an unknown amount of 2C-T-21. ... He developed a temperature of 108 degrees Fahrenheit (42 degrees Celsius), had a tonic-clonic seizure, and slipped into a coma before dying four days later."

"It is strongly discouraged to administer 2C-T-21 non-orally because this may cause vomiting, numerous negative side effects, or death at high doses."
