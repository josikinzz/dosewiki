## Source: Wikipedia

Common side effects include weakness, sleepiness, dizziness, decreased alertness, decreased memory formation, low blood pressure, and a decreased effort to breathe.

Lorazepam can effectively reduce agitation and induce sleep...

Adverse effects can include sedation and low blood pressure... Other adverse effects include confusion, ataxia, inhibiting the formation of new memories, pupil constriction, and hangover effects. ... Lorazepam appears to have more profound adverse effects on memory than other benzodiazepines; it impairs both explicit and implicit memory. ... Paradoxical effects can also occur, such as worsening of seizures, or paradoxical excitement; paradoxical excitement is more likely to occur in the elderly, children, those with a history of alcohol abuse, and in people with a history of aggression or anger problems.

In a group of around 3,500 people treated for anxiety, the most common side effects complained of from lorazepam were sedation (15.9%), dizziness (6.9%), weakness (4.2%), and unsteadiness (3.4%). ... Behavioral disinhibition and respiratory depression as well as hypotension may also occur.

In some cases, paradoxical effects can occur... such as increased hostility, aggression, angry outbursts, and psychomotor agitation. These effects are seen more commonly with lorazepam than with other benzodiazepines.

Among benzodiazepines, lorazepam has relatively strong amnesic effects... memory gaps for parts of [the] day.

Other adverse effects include: Slurred speech, Gastrointestinal disturbances, lasting 12 or more hours, Vomiting, Respiratory depression in higher doses.
