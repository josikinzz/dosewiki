## Source: PsychonautWiki

4-AcO-DET ( 4-Acetoxy-N,N-diethyltryptamine , ethacetin ) is a lesser-known novel [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) substance of the [tryptamine](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Tryptamine) class. It is chemically related to [psilocin](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psilocin) and part of a series of substituted tryptamines that includes 4-AcO-MET and 4-AcO-DMT.

There is very little data on the human pharmacology or toxicity of 4-AcO-DET, although analytical methods have been developed for its detection.

### Pharmacology

4-substituted acetylated tryptamines such as 4-AcO-DET, [4-AcO-MET](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-AcO-MET) , and [4-AcO-DMT](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-AcO-DMT) are hypothesized to principally act as a [prodrug](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Prodrug) for their respective hydrolyzed counterparts (e.g. [4-HO-DMT](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-HO-DMT) , [4-HO-MET](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-HO-MET) and [4-HO-DET](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/4-HO-DET) ). In theory, they would become inactive until they are deacetylated in the body, although there is on-going discussion as to whether they might display their own intrinsic activity. [*[citation needed](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Citation_needed)*]

Like with most [psychedelic](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psychedelic) tryptamines, 4-AcO-DET is thought to act principally as a [5-HT2A](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Serotonin#The_5-HT_system) [partial agonist](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Agonist#Agonists) . The psychedelic effects are believed to come from 4-AcO-DET's binding efficacy at the 5-HT 2A receptors.

However, the role of these interactions and how they result in the psychedelic experience continues to remain elusive.

### Pharmacology

It is expected that 4-AcO-DET is quickly hydrolyzed into the free phenolic 4-HO-DET by serum esterases, but human studies concerning the metabolic fate of this drug are lacking.
