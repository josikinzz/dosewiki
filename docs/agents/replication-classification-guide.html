Dose Wiki · future intake protocol

Classify the record in context. Then tag what the media depicts.

A replication does not need to resemble a synthetic first-person overlay. An ordinary-looking scene, illustrative example, or traditional psychedelic artwork can be a replication when the source library assigns it that role or an effect.

Context-first admission

Use attributable source role and effect assignment to decide whether an item belongs to the replication corpus.

Mechanism-evidence tags

Plate 01 · deterministic procedure

Use the same order for every incoming work.

Bind the record, recover its context, make the narrow exclusion decision, and then classify the complete media.

01

Bind identity and provenance

Record stable ID, slug, title, credited artist, media type, source path or URL, storage ID, and current taxonomy digest.

Complete when

The stable ID resolves exactly once and the inspected asset matches the record.

02

Recover source context

Read the source role, assigned effect slug, collection, caption, and article placement before deciding gallery status.

Complete when

You can state whether the source calls it a replication, assigns an effect, or treats it as support material.

03

Apply context-first admission

Use the gallery admission criteria for an explicit figure or concrete item-specific exclusion; preserve source-assigned illustrative and traditional works.

Complete when

The status has a concrete rationale and any exclusion remains visible in review data.

04

Inspect the complete media

Open images at full resolution. For video, play the full sequence and sample distributed frames across every material phase.

Complete when

You can describe the persistent scene, imagined field, transitions, and disputed phases.

05

Assign viewing mode and content family

Choose open-eye, closed-eye, or mixed. Record the content family separately from gallery status.

Complete when

Every replication has a mode and the rationale identifies the external substrate or explains why none persists.

06

Extract title-only drug evidence

Apply the title-only drug criteria, preserving the named drug and broad class separately from visual-effect evidence.

Complete when

Every drug tag preserves the exact matched title text and a normalized slug.

07

Classify the artist practice

Use the artist's reviewed corpus to choose replicator, traditional psychedelic artist, mixed, other, or uncertain. Keep this independent of the current item's status.

Complete when

The artist result cites corpus evidence and uncertainty remains explicit.

08

Record history and promote safely

Preserve prior decisions, evidence inspected, review count, reconciliation basis, and expected live identity. Validate locally before any database write.

Complete when

A later agent can replay the decision and prove the intended row received it.

Plate 02 · gallery admission

not-replication is a narrow exclusion.

It means the item is genuinely outside the replication corpus. It does not mean “the perceptual effect is not obvious from the pixels alone.”

✓
Current precedence

An explicit source role of replication or a non-empty assigned effect_slug establishes item-level replication context unless the item is an explicit figure.

1 · context

Read the source

Role, effect assignment, placement, caption, provenance.

2 · figure gate

Exclude support figures

Diagram, chart, trace, molecule figure, or clearly non-gallery support asset.

3 · admission

Honor replication context

Illustrative and traditional works remain eligible when assigned as replications.

4 · uncertainty

Keep it visible

If context is insufficient, record unclear; do not silently hide it.

Plate 03 · viewing mode

Ask whether an external-world substrate persists.

Viewing mode describes the depicted experience. It is not a quality score and it is not synonymous with an artist category.

open-eye

The world remains.

  • A recognizable room, landscape, person, object, photograph, or filmed scene persists.
  • Distortions, overlays, enhancements, tracers, faces, or other effects act on that substrate.
  • An ordinary example may qualify when source context identifies what it demonstrates.
closed-eye

The vision owns the field.

  • No persistent external scene anchors the image or sequence.
  • Geometry, tunnels, hyperspace fields, imagined settings, entities, or visionary scenes fill the frame.
  • Traditional psychedelic, cultural, surreal, and generative art commonly lands here.
!
Use mixed sparingly.

Choose it only when materially distinct open-eye and closed-eye phases both appear. A surreal overlay on a visible room is still open-eye; ornament inside a self-contained vision is still closed-eye.

Plate 04 · title evidence

Drug tags come from the title, literally.

These tags support filtering. A visually plausible substance guess is still a false data claim.

psychedelics

Classic psychedelics

Normalize an explicitly named psychedelic and store its matched title text.

dissociatives

Dissociatives

Use when the explicit title drug belongs to the dissociative class.

deliriants

Deliriants

The canonical class value is deliriants. Do not invent a singular or alternate enum.

other

Atypical and other

Keep the exact drug tag even when it is atypical. For example, explicit Salvia receives drug salvia and class other.

reject

No visual inference

Do not derive a drug from the aesthetic, effects, artist, source directory, description, article, or neighboring items.

evidence

Preserve the match

Store normalized slug, display name, class, and matched_title_text. Longest non-contained exact matches win.

Plate 05 · artist practice

Artist practice and item status are separate axes.

A traditional psychedelic artist can make a replication. A replicator can make a support asset. Classify the corpus and the row independently.

replicator

Replicator

The reviewed practice substantially recreates or demonstrates subjective effects, often through altered real-world scenes.

traditional-psychedelic-artist

Traditional psychedelic artist

The reviewed practice centers visionary, symbolic, surreal, cultural, or self-contained psychedelic art.

mixed

Mixed

Both practices are materially established. Do not use mixed merely because one work is hard to classify.

other

Other

Attributable practice is established but does not fit either requested artist category.

uncertain

Uncertain

The available corpus cannot establish a stable practice without guessing. Keep the review flag.

boundary

No reputation override

Artist reputation may support practice classification. It must not override the source context or evidence for an individual item.

Plate 06 · campaign corrections

The failure mode was stripping context too early.

Historical 2026-08-25 campaign evidence: an independent visual pass over-excluded 106 items and left two unclear. All 108 carried source replication context and were restored. These counts are not a current inventory.

Admit illustrative works through source context.

A stock photograph, ordinary clip, or illustrative example may demonstrate an assigned effect. effect-illustration and not-replication are independent decisions.

Recognize mechanisms on ordinary substrates.

Cloud, tree, and roof faces can represent increased pareidolia. Tumbling dice can represent tracers. A chameleon or garden can demonstrate colour enhancement.

Context can make a subtle mechanism legible.

A Romanesco can illustrate geometry. Slow-motion footage can represent visual-processing acceleration. A forest or wolf sequence can represent drifting.

Traditional art can depict closed-eye experience.

Visionary, cultural, generative, and dark-surrealist work remains replication content when the source assigns it that role. Style alone neither admits nor excludes it.

Keep exclusions concrete.

The historical reconciled corpus retained 26 explicit source figures plus the manually adjudicated Eye Movement Diagram as not-replications; future decisions require their own evidence.

Preserve the superseded decision.

When context changes a status, record the prior status, basis, rationale, source role, effect slug, and reconciliation signals in contextReconciliation.

Plate 07 · machine-readable record

Every decision needs an address and an explanation.

Keep the manifest replayable. Use exact enum strings from src/types/replications.ts; do not create near-synonyms.

Status, confidence, review-required flag, and concrete rationale.

What kind of media it is, separate from whether it belongs in the gallery.

The rule or review stage that owns the final result.

Corpus-level practice classification, confidence, rationale, count, and review-required state.
×
Never overwrite silently.

A classification correction is new evidence, not permission to erase the previous reasoning. History is what makes later review and safe production promotion possible.

Plate 08 · ledgered promotion

Move from evidence to production in explicit gates.

Local classification does not authorize a production write. A login does not prove the intended Postgres target. Every write must bind target, digest, identity, and ledger.

1 · ProduceBuild the ID-keyed local manifest from attributable source data and reviewed media.
2 · ValidateParse JSON, check exact coverage, unique IDs, allowed enums, required modes, and referenced media.
3 · PlanRun the importer dry. Review counts, expected identities, source digest, collisions, and target URL.
4 · CanaryWrite a tiny representative set, read it back, and record verification in the new ledger.
5 · ResumeUse a previous ledger only when it verified the exact row and still owns the live digest.
6 · CompleteWrite pending rows and artists, read back every batch, rebuild both public sites, and inspect served behavior.
!
Publication gate

Only explicit not-replication rows are hidden by taxonomy. unclear, unreviewed, and absent statuses remain visible by default so uncertainty cannot disappear silently.

Plate 09 · finish line

Do not call the intake complete until every gate passes.

Counts embedded in reports are dated snapshots. Recompute them from the current structured manifest and live readback.

  • Every incoming media record has one stable, unique identity.
  • Every row has status, confidence, rationale, basis, and evidence.
  • Every confirmed replication has open-eye, closed-eye, or mixed mode.
  • Every hidden item has an explicit, concrete not-replication rationale.
  • Illustrative and traditional works were checked against source context.
  • Unclear items and artist-review flags remain visible.
  • Drug tags use explicit title text and canonical classes only.
  • Artist practice is classified independently from item status.
  • Modified JSON parses and all referenced media exists.
  • Dry run matches exact row, artist, status, and mode counts.
  • Canary and full write are ledgered and read back by identity plus digest.
  • Both public builds show restored examples and hide only true exclusions.
