### Pharmacology

**DALT and other extended N,N-dialkyltryptamines produce similar effects to dimethyltryptamine (DMT) but are longer-lasting, with durations of up to 3 hours.**

**The drug produces the head-twitch response, a behavioral proxy of psychedelic effects, in rodents.**
